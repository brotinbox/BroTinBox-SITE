> **Quick answer:** For a quick **sweet corn salad**, boil or steam **1 cup sweet corn kernels** for 4–5 minutes, cool, then toss with **diced capsicum, onion, tomato, cucumber, coriander, lemon juice, salt, pepper and a pinch of chaat masala**. A **100 g serving** has about **97 kcal, 3 g protein and 2.8 g fibre**, our kitchen's numbers. It is a fresh, colourful side; pair it with a **protein main** to make a full meal.

Sweet corn salad is the bright, crunchy side in many of our BroTin Box meals. It takes 10 minutes, kids love it, and it adds colour, fibre and freshness to any high-protein plate.

# Ingredients (serves 2)

- 1 cup sweet corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- ½ capsicum (red, yellow or green), finely diced
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 small tomato, deseeded and chopped
- ½ cucumber, diced
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
- 2 tbsp chopped coriander
- 1 tbsp chopped mint (optional)
- Juice of ½–1 lemon
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp chaat masala (optional)
- ¼ tsp roasted cumin powder
- Salt to taste

# Method

## 1. Cook the corn

- **Boil** the corn in salted water for **4–5 minutes**, or **steam** it for 5–6 minutes.
- Drain and spread on a plate to cool.

**Tip:** for a smoky taste, toss boiled corn in a hot pan with a few drops of butter or oil for 2 minutes.

## 2. Chop the vegetables

Dice everything small and roughly the same size as the corn kernels.

## 3. Toss

In a bowl, mix the corn and vegetables. Add pepper, cumin, chaat masala and salt.

## 4. Finish

**Add lemon juice just before serving** so the vegetables stay crisp. Top with coriander and mint.

# Nutrition

For **100 g** (like our kitchen's Sweet Corn Salad):

- **Calories:** about 97 kcal
- **Protein:** about 3 g
- **Carbs:** about 19 g
- **Fibre:** about 2.8 g
- **Fat:** about 1 g

# Make it a full meal

Sweet corn salad is a side, not a main. Add protein:

- **+ 150 g grilled chicken:** about 45 g protein. See [mint grilled chicken](/blog/mint-grilled-chicken-recipe).
- **+ 100 g paneer cubes:** about 18–20 g
- **+ 2 boiled eggs:** about 12 g
- **+ a cup of black chana or rajma:** about 8–13 g. See [black chana sundal](/blog/black-chana-sundal-recipe).

**Example bowl:** sweet corn salad + smashed chicken + 2 boiled eggs = about 450 kcal and 60 g protein.

# Variations

- **Corn and sprouts:** add a cup of cooked green gram sprouts for protein
- **Corn chaat:** add a spoon of curd, tamarind chutney and sev (more calories)
- **Butter masala corn:** tossed with 1 tsp butter, chilli powder and lemon, like the street stall version
- **Corn and paneer salad:** add 50 g paneer cubes

# Is sweet corn good for weight loss?

Yes, in a side portion. Sweet corn is a **starchy vegetable**, so it has more carbs than leafy salads, but it is filling, has fibre and makes salads more enjoyable. Watch butter and cheese at street stalls.

# Tips

- **Frozen corn works well;** thaw and boil briefly
- **Add salt and lemon at the end** to prevent a watery salad
- **Store** chopped vegetables and corn separately; mix just before eating
- **Keeps** for a few hours in the fridge once dressed

# Frequently asked questions

**Is sweet corn high in protein?**
No. It has about 3 g per 100 g. Pair it with a protein food.

**Can diabetics eat sweet corn salad?**
In a small portion with protein and vegetables, many can. Follow your doctor's advice.

**Is sweet corn the same as regular corn?**
Sweet corn is a variety picked young and sweet. Field corn (for flour) is different.
