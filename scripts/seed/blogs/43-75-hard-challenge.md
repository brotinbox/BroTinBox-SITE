> **Quick answer:** **75 Hard** is a 75-day discipline challenge created by US entrepreneur Andy Frisella in 2019. Every day you must: **do two 45-minute workouts (one outdoors)**, **follow a diet with no cheat meals or alcohol**, **drink about 3.8 litres (1 gallon) of water**, **read 10 pages of a non-fiction book**, and **take a progress photo**. Miss any task and you start again from day 1. It can work for discipline, but it is very demanding. Many people do better with a softer version.

75 Hard has been all over Instagram for years, and it peaks every year around New Year and the winter arc season. Here are the rules, how to do it in India, and whether it is a good idea for you.

# The 5 rules of 75 Hard

Every single day, for 75 days in a row:

1. **Two workouts of at least 45 minutes each.** One must be outdoors, whatever the weather.
2. **Follow a diet.** Any diet you choose, but no cheat meals and no alcohol.
3. **Drink about 3.8 litres of water** (1 US gallon).
4. **Read 10 pages** of a non-fiction or self-improvement book. Audiobooks do not count in the original rules.
5. **Take a progress photo.**

If you miss any one of these on any day, you **restart from day 1**.

# Why people like it

- **Clear rules.** No guessing, no "I will see how I feel today".
- **All-or-nothing.** For some personalities, this works better than "do your best".
- **Visible results.** Two workouts a day plus a clean diet usually leads to fat loss.
- **Confidence.** Finishing 75 strict days feels like a real achievement.

# The problems with 75 Hard

It was designed as a **mental toughness** programme, not a fitness or nutrition plan. Some issues:

- **Two workouts a day for 75 days** is a lot of volume, especially for beginners. Injuries and burnout are common.
- **"Any diet" can be a bad diet.** Some people pick extreme, very low-calorie diets and lose muscle.
- **3.8 litres of water** is fine for most healthy adults, but it is not right for everyone. People with heart or kidney conditions should not follow this without medical advice.
- **The restart rule** can turn one bad day into quitting the whole thing.
- **No rest days.** Rest is when your body actually recovers and adapts.

# How to do 75 Hard in India

If you decide to do it, a few practical tips:

## Workouts
- Make one of the two workouts **low intensity**: a 45-minute brisk walk counts.
- The outdoor rule is easier in Coimbatore's cooler months (November to February). Walk early morning or after sunset.
- During heavy rain, walk under cover or wear a raincoat. The original rules say outdoors means outdoors.

## Diet
Choose a diet you can actually sustain for 75 days:
- High protein (about 1.6 g per kg)
- A modest calorie deficit (300–500 kcal), not a crash diet
- Mostly whole foods: rice or chapati in measured portions, dal, vegetables, eggs, chicken, fish, paneer
- Plan for weddings, festivals and family functions. Our [wedding season guide](/blog/wedding-season-diet-tamil-nadu) helps.

## Water
Spread it through the day. Carry a 1-litre bottle and refill it about four times.

## Reading
10 pages takes 15–20 minutes. Do it at the same time each day, like before bed instead of scrolling.

# A softer version: "75 Soft" or "75 Medium"

Many people now do a more sustainable version:

- **One 45-minute workout a day**, with one active recovery day a week
- **Eat well**, allowing one social meal a week
- **2.5–3 litres of water**
- **Read 10 pages**
- **No restart rule**: just don't miss two days in a row

For most people with jobs, college or family, this version builds better long-term habits. It is also close to what a good [winter arc](/blog/what-is-winter-arc) looks like.

# Results you can expect

With two workouts a day and a sensible deficit, many people lose about **4–8 kg over 75 days**, along with big improvements in fitness and routine. Results depend heavily on your starting point and how good your diet is.

# Who should not do 75 Hard

- People with heart conditions, kidney disease or other medical conditions (without a doctor's approval)
- Anyone with a history of eating disorders
- Complete beginners with no training background (start with 3–4 workouts a week instead)
- Pregnant women

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I do 75 Hard without a gym?**
Yes. Walking, running, home workouts, yoga and cycling all count as workouts.

**Do the two workouts need a gap?**
The original rules say they must be separate sessions. Most people do one in the morning and one in the evening.

**Can I drink tea or coffee?**
Yes. The rules only ban alcohol and cheat meals. Watch the sugar.

**What diet should I follow?**
Any balanced diet with enough protein and a moderate calorie deficit. Avoid extreme diets.
