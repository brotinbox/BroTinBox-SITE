> **Quick answer:** **Intermittent fasting (IF)** means eating within a set time window, most commonly **16:8** (eat for 8 hours, fast for 16). On an Indian diet, a simple version is eating between **12 PM and 8 PM**, with lunch, an evening snack and dinner. IF helps weight loss mainly because it **reduces total calories**; studies show results similar to regular calorie counting. It is not suitable for everyone, especially people with **diabetes on medicines, pregnant women, or anyone with a history of eating disorders**.

Intermittent fasting is one of the most popular diets in India today. It fits well with Indian habits, since many people already skip breakfast or eat a late dinner. Here is how to do it properly with Indian food.

# Types of intermittent fasting

- **16:8:** fast 16 hours, eat within 8 hours. The most popular.
- **14:10:** fast 14 hours, eat within 10. Easier for beginners and women.
- **5:2:** eat normally 5 days a week, and about 500–600 kcal on 2 days.
- **18:6 or OMAD (one meal a day):** more extreme, harder to get enough protein.

Start with **14:10** and move to 16:8 if it suits you.

# How intermittent fasting helps weight loss

- **Fewer eating occasions = fewer calories** for most people
- **Stops late-night snacking**, a big source of extra calories
- **Simple rule** that some people find easier than counting calories

What it does **not** do: burn fat by magic. If you overeat in your 8-hour window, you will not lose weight. Studies comparing IF with normal calorie restriction usually find **similar weight loss**.

# A 16:8 Indian meal plan (12 PM to 8 PM)

**Before 12 PM (fasting):** water, black coffee, plain tea without milk or sugar, green tea

**12 PM, lunch (big meal)**

- 1 cup rice or 2 chapatis
- 150 g chicken, fish, paneer or tofu, or 2 eggs plus dal
- Vegetables or poriyal, salad
- Curd

**4 PM, snack**

- Buttermilk and sundal, or fruit with a handful of peanuts

**7:30 PM, dinner**

- 2 chapatis or 2–3 idlis
- Dal, egg curry or chicken
- Vegetables

**After 8 PM:** water only

This gives about **1,500–1,800 kcal and 80–100 g protein**, depending on portions.

# Tips for doing IF with Indian food

- **Break the fast with protein, not sweets or fried snacks.** A high-protein lunch keeps you full until evening.
- **Protein in every meal:** with fewer meals, each one needs more. Aim for 30–40 g.
- **Do not make tea with sugar** during the fast. Milk tea breaks the fast too; have it in your eating window.
- **Plan for functions and family dinners:** shift your window on those days.
- **Drink enough water,** especially in hot weather.

# Is it okay to skip breakfast?

For most healthy adults, yes. There is no strong evidence that breakfast itself is needed for weight loss. What matters is your total food over the day.

If you train in the morning, you may feel better eating after your workout. Read [what to eat before and after a workout](/blog/what-to-eat-before-and-after-workout).

# Who should not do intermittent fasting without a doctor

- **Diabetes**, especially on insulin or sulfonylureas (risk of low sugar)
- **Pregnant or breastfeeding** women
- **History of eating disorders**
- **Underweight** people
- **Teenagers**
- People on medicines that must be taken with food

Women with PCOS or thyroid conditions can often do IF, but should discuss it with their doctor.

# Common mistakes

- **Overeating in the window** because you feel you "earned" it
- **Too little protein** across fewer meals
- **Ending the fast with biryani or parotta** every day
- **Very long fasts** (20+ hours) too soon
- **Thinking IF makes calories not count**

# IF vs a regular calorie deficit

Both work if you eat less than you burn. Choose IF if:

- You are not hungry in the morning
- You tend to snack late at night
- You like simple rules

Choose regular meals if:

- You get very hungry and then overeat
- You train early and need breakfast
- You have a medical condition that needs regular meals

# Frequently asked questions

**Does black coffee break the fast?**
No. Black coffee, plain tea and water are fine. Milk and sugar break it.

**Can I lift weights while fasting?**
Yes. Many people train at the end of their fast and eat right after.

**How much weight can I lose with 16:8?**
About the same as any diet with the same deficit, usually 0.5–1 kg a week.
