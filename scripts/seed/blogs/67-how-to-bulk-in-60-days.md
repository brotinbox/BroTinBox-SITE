> **Quick answer:** In 60 days, a beginner can usually gain about **2–4 kg on the scale**, with roughly **1–2 kg of that as muscle**, by eating **300–500 kcal above maintenance**, taking **1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg**, and following a progressive weight training plan **4–5 days a week**. Split the 60 days into two phases: build the habit in month 1, then push your training and adjust calories in month 2.

Sixty days is the sweet spot for a first bulk. It is long enough to see real changes in the mirror and in your lifts, but short enough to stay focused. Here is a week-by-week plan.

# What 60 days can realistically do

For a beginner to intermediate lifter:

- **Scale weight:** +2–4 kg
- **Muscle:** about +1–2 kg (more if you are new to training)
- **Strength:** big jumps on squat, bench and rows
- **Look:** clearly bigger arms, shoulders, chest and legs, especially if you started skinny

If you have trained for years, expect about half of that. Muscle gain slows as you get more experienced.

# Before you start: 3 numbers

## 1. Maintenance calories

Track your normal eating for a week. If your weight stays the same, that is maintenance. Or estimate:

- Body weight (kg) × 30–33 for a sitting job and 3–4 training days
- Body weight (kg) × 34–38 for an active job or 5–6 training days

## 2. Your bulking calories

Maintenance **+ 300–500 kcal**. Use the higher end if you are very skinny.

## 3. Your protein target

**1.6–2.2 g per kg.** For 65 kg, about 105–140 g a day.

Use our guide on [calories to build muscle](/blog/calories-to-build-muscle) if you want a more exact number.

# Month 1 (days 1–30): build the base

**Food**

- Eat at your bulking calories every day, including rest days
- 4 meals a day with 25–40 g protein each
- Add easy calories: milk, peanut butter, bananas, curd rice, extra chapati

**Training**

- 4 days a week: upper / lower / upper / lower, or a [push pull legs plan](/blog/push-pull-legs-workout-plan)
- Focus on form in the big lifts
- Add a little weight or 1–2 reps every week

**Goal by day 30:** +1–2 kg on the scale, all main lifts up.

Our [30-day bulking guide](/blog/how-to-bulk-in-30-days) covers month 1 in detail.

# Month 2 (days 31–60): push harder

**Adjust calories**

- Gained less than 1 kg in month 1? Add **200 kcal a day**.
- Gained more than 2.5 kg and your waist grew a lot? Cut **150–200 kcal**.
- In the right range? Keep going.

**Training**

- Move to 5 days a week if you can recover
- Add 1–2 sets for lagging muscles (often legs, back, shoulders)
- Keep adding weight to your main lifts

**Goal by day 60:** +2–4 kg in total, strength clearly higher.

# A sample 2,600 kcal bulking day

For a 65 kg person:

- **Breakfast:** 4-egg omelette, 2 chapatis, a glass of milk
- **Mid-morning:** banana and peanut butter on toast
- **Lunch:** 150 g chicken, 1.5 cups rice, dal, vegetables, curd
- **Pre-workout:** 2 bananas or poha
- **Post-workout:** a homemade protein shake or sundal
- **Dinner:** fish or paneer, 2–3 chapatis, vegetables
- **Before bed:** a glass of milk or curd

About **2,600 kcal and 130–140 g protein**. Vegetarians can use soya chunks, paneer, tofu and extra dal. See [Indian gym diet for muscle gain](/blog/indian-gym-diet-muscle-gain) for more meal ideas.

# Track your bulk every week

- **Weight:** weigh 3–4 mornings and use the weekly average. Aim for **+0.25–0.5 kg a week**.
- **Waist:** measure at the navel every 2 weeks. If it grows fast while lifts stall, you are adding mostly fat.
- **Lifts:** write them down. Strength going up = muscle going up.
- **Photos:** day 1, day 30, day 60, same light and pose.

# Mistakes that ruin a 60-day bulk

- **Eating big only on training days.** Muscle grows on rest days too.
- **Dirty bulking** on parotta, biryani and sweets. Read [dirty bulk vs clean bulk](/blog/dirty-bulk-vs-clean-bulk).
- **Changing your program every week.** Stick to one plan for the full 60 days.
- **Not sleeping.** Aim for 7–8 hours.
- **Giving up in week 3** because the mirror has not changed yet. It will.

# After day 60

You have three choices:

- **Keep bulking** if you are still lean and gaining well
- **Take 2–4 weeks at maintenance** to settle in
- **Do a short cut** if you gained more fat than you wanted. See [bulking vs cutting](/blog/bulking-vs-cutting).

# Frequently asked questions

**Is 60 days enough to see results?**
Yes. Most people see clear changes in strength, weight and how their clothes fit.

**Do I need whey or creatine?**
No, but both can help. Read [is creatine good for health](/blog/is-creatine-good-for-health).

**Can I bulk on a veg diet?**
Yes. Paneer, soya, tofu, curd, milk, dal and chana can cover your protein.
