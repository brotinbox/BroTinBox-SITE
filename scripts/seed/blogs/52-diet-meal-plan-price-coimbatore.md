# How it compares

## vs cooking at home

Cooking your own high-protein meals is cheaper on ingredients. For a chicken, egg and dal based lunch, ingredients alone can cost around ₹120–180 per meal in Coimbatore, depending on the cut and portions.

What you pay for with a meal plan:

- Planning and shopping
- Cooking and cleaning time (often 1–1.5 hours a day)
- Measured portions and macros
- Consistency on busy days

We worked through the full comparison in [meal prep vs meal subscription](/blog/meal-prep-vs-meal-subscription-cost).

## vs eating out

A typical lunch out in Coimbatore:

- Meals plate: ₹120–200, but low in protein
- Chicken biryani: ₹200–350, often 700–1,000 kcal
- Grilled chicken plus sides at a "healthy" café: ₹300–450

A measured ₹269 box with known protein and calories usually costs about the same as, or less than, a protein-heavy restaurant meal.
