> **Quick answer:** You can make a **20–30 g protein shake without protein powder** using **milk, thick curd, paneer, peanut butter, sattu, oats or soya milk**. For example, 300 ml milk + 100 g hung curd + 1 tablespoon peanut butter + 1 banana gives about **25 g of protein** and about 450 kcal. These shakes cost less than whey and add useful calories for muscle gain.

No whey at home? Do not want to spend ₹3,000+ on a tub? You can still make a solid protein shake from everyday Indian kitchen ingredients.

# High-protein ingredients you already have

Protein per typical amount:

- **Milk (300 ml, toned):** about 9–10 g
- **Thick hung curd (100 g):** about 8–10 g
- **Regular curd (100 g):** about 3–4 g
- **Paneer (50 g):** about 9–10 g
- **Peanut butter (1 tablespoon, 16 g):** about 4 g
- **Roasted peanuts (20 g):** about 5 g
- **Sattu, roasted chana flour (30 g):** about 6–7 g
- **Oats (30 g):** about 4 g
- **Soya milk (250 ml):** about 7–8 g
- **Chia or flax seeds (1 tablespoon):** about 2 g

# 5 protein shake recipes without powder

## 1. Classic banana peanut butter shake (about 25 g protein)
- 300 ml milk
- 100 g hung curd
- 1 banana
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter
- A pinch of cinnamon

**About 450 kcal.** Great after the gym or as breakfast on the go.

## 2. Sattu power drink (about 20 g protein)
Sattu is roasted chana flour. It is a traditional energy drink in North India and is now easy to find in Coimbatore supermarkets.

- 300 ml milk
- 3 tablespoons (about 40 g) sattu
- 2 dates (for sweetness)
- A pinch of cardamom

**About 400 kcal.** For a savoury version, mix sattu with water, lemon, salt and roasted jeera.

## 3. Paneer mango or chikoo smoothie (about 22 g protein)
- 250 ml milk
- 50 g fresh paneer
- 1 cup mango or chikoo
- 1 teaspoon honey (optional)

**About 400 kcal.** Paneer blends surprisingly smooth with fruit and milk.

## 4. Oats and almond shake (about 18–20 g protein)
- 300 ml milk
- 30 g oats (soaked for 10 minutes)
- 8–10 almonds or 1 tablespoon almond butter
- 1 banana

**About 480 kcal.** Good for bulking and slow-release energy.

## 5. Vegan soya shake (about 15–18 g protein)
- 300 ml unsweetened soya milk
- 1 tablespoon peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon chia seeds
- 1 banana or a handful of berries

**About 380 kcal.** A good option for people who avoid dairy.

# Tips for better homemade shakes

- **Use hung curd.** It doubles the protein compared with regular curd. Hang regular curd in a muslin cloth for 2–3 hours.
- **Chill the milk** or add ice for a thicker texture.
- **Sweeten with fruit or dates** instead of sugar.
- **Make it lighter for weight loss:** use toned or skimmed milk, skip peanut butter and dates.
- **Make it heavier for bulking:** add oats, more peanut butter, and full-cream milk.

# Homemade shakes vs whey

**Homemade shakes**
- Cheaper per serving
- Give carbs, healthy fats, fibre and vitamins too
- Higher in calories, which helps bulking
- Take 5 minutes to make

**Whey**
- More protein for fewer calories (about 24 g per scoop for about 120 kcal)
- Very fast and easy
- Better for people on a strict calorie limit

Neither is required. Most people can hit their protein target from regular meals. Read [protein powder vs real food](/blog/protein-powder-vs-real-food-india) and [is whey protein enough to build muscle](/blog/is-whey-protein-enough-to-build-muscle).

# When to drink a protein shake

- **After a workout**, as part of your post-workout meal
- **At breakfast** when you are short on time
- **As an evening snack** before the gym (use a lighter version)

Timing matters less than your total protein for the day. See [what to eat before and after a workout](/blog/what-to-eat-before-and-after-workout).

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I build muscle with homemade shakes?**
Yes, as part of a diet with enough total protein and calories, and proper training.

**Is raw egg in a shake a good idea?**
No. Raw eggs carry a risk of salmonella, and the protein is absorbed less well than from cooked eggs.

**Can I add peanut butter if I am losing weight?**
Yes, but measure it. One tablespoon is about 95 kcal. See [peanut butter for muscle gain](/blog/peanut-butter-for-muscle-gain).

**Is sattu good for weight loss?**
Sattu is high in protein and fibre and keeps you full. Use measured portions.
