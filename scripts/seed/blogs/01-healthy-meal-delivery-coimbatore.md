# The 8-point checklist for choosing a healthy meal subscription

## 1. Does every box show calories, protein, carbs and fat?

If a service cannot tell you how much protein is in today's box, it cannot help you hit a goal. Look for numbers per box, not vague words like "protein-rich" or "balanced". At BroTin Box, every box on our [weekly menu](/menu) lists **calories, protein, carbs and fat**, along with the weight of each item.

## 2. Are portions weighed or guessed?

A ladle of curry can vary by 50 g from one serving to the next. A spoon of oil can add 100+ calories without anyone noticing. Ask whether the kitchen weighs ingredients on digital scales. We weigh every component, so the numbers on the label match what is actually in your box within a normal **±5–8% natural variance**.

## 3. Is it cooked fresh the same day?

Many "meal prep" brands cook in bulk and refrigerate for days. Fresh food tastes better and you do not need to wonder how long it has been sitting. Our kitchen cooks every batch the same day it is delivered. Nothing is pre-packed days in advance, and there are no added preservatives.

## 4. Is the food something you will still eat in week three?

This is where most diet plans fail. Boiled chicken and plain broccoli works for four days, then you order a biryani. Food that people stick with tastes like home: green mint chicken, oats pongal, sprouted green gram, kollu, grilled mackerel. Taste is not a luxury. It decides whether the plan works.

## 5. What oil do they use, and how much?

Oil is the biggest hidden source of calories in Indian cooking. Ask what oil is used and whether it is measured. We cook with **cold-pressed sesame and groundnut oils in measured grams**, never free-poured.

## 6. Are delivery times fixed?

If your breakfast arrives at 11 AM, it is not breakfast. A good service gives you a fixed window. We deliver **Monday to Saturday**, in two slots: **7:00–9:00 AM** for your morning box and **7:00–9:00 PM** for dinner.

## 7. Can you pause or skip without losing money?

Travel, family functions and sick days happen. Check the pause rules before you subscribe. With BroTin Box you can **skip or pause any day up to 9:00 PM the night before**, and unused days roll forward instead of disappearing.

## 8. Can you try a single box before committing?

Never sign up for a month of food you have not tasted. A one-day trial removes the risk. Our [daily trial box](/tomorrow) costs **₹289 for veg and ₹319 for non-veg**, with no subscription required.

# Red flags to watch for

- **No numbers at all.** "Healthy", "clean" and "diet" are not nutrition information.
- **Calories printed but no protein.** Protein is the number that keeps you full and protects your muscle while you lose fat.
- **Very low calories in every box.** A 350 kcal dinner looks great on paper but leaves most adults hungry by 10 PM, which leads to snacking.
- **Unclear delivery areas or fees.** You should know before checkout whether your area is covered and what delivery costs.
- **Long lock-in periods with no pause option.**
