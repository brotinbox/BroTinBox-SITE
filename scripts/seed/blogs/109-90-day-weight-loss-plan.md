> **Quick answer:** In **90 days**, many people with weight to lose can safely drop about **6–12 kg**, depending on their starting weight, at roughly **0.5–1% of body weight a week**. Split the 90 days into three 30-day phases: **build habits** (calorie deficit, protein, walking), **build momentum** (strength training, more steps, adjust calories), and **lock it in** (handle plateaus and plan for maintenance). A one-week break at maintenance around day 60 can make the plan easier to stick to.

Ninety days is long enough to change your body and your habits. Here is a phase-by-phase plan with Indian food that you can actually follow.

# What to expect in 90 days

At a safe rate of about 0.5–1% of body weight a week:

- **70 kg:** about 4–7 kg
- **85 kg:** about 5–9 kg
- **100 kg:** about 6–11 kg
- **120 kg:** about 7–13 kg

Plus a smaller waist, better energy and stronger lifts.

# Before day 1: set up

- **Weigh yourself** 3 mornings and average it
- **Measure your waist** at the navel
- **Take front, side and back photos**
- **Estimate your calories:** see [how many calories should I eat](/blog/how-many-calories-should-i-eat)
- **Set a target:** maintenance minus about 500 kcal
- **Set a protein goal:** 1.2–1.6 g per kg

# Phase 1 (days 1–30): build the habits

**Food**

- Use the plate method: half vegetables, a quarter protein, a quarter rice or chapati
- Protein at every meal
- Cut sugar in tea, juices, fried snacks and late-night eating
- Measure oil and rice for 2 weeks to learn portions

**Movement**

- Build up to **8,000 steps a day**
- **2 strength sessions a week** (gym or home)

**Expect:** 2–4 kg down, with the first week faster (water).

Details: [how to lose weight in 30 days](/blog/how-to-lose-weight-in-30-days).

# Phase 2 (days 31–60): build momentum

**Food**

- Keep the same structure
- Plan for weekends, functions and eating out
- Add more vegetables and protein when hungry, not more rice

**Movement**

- **8,000–10,000 steps a day**
- **3 strength sessions a week**
- Optional: 1–2 cardio sessions

**Adjust:** if your weekly average has not moved for 2 weeks, cut 150–200 kcal or add 2,000 steps.

**Expect:** about 0.5–1 kg a week.

# Optional diet break (around day 60)

Eat at **maintenance for 5–7 days**. Keep protein high and keep walking.

Why: it gives you a mental break, can help with hunger and energy, and makes the final phase easier. You may see a small water gain; it drops when you return to the deficit.

# Phase 3 (days 61–90): lock it in

**Food**

- Recalculate calories for your new weight (you need slightly less now)
- Keep protein high
- Practise "maintenance meals" a couple of times a week so you learn what eating normally looks like

**Movement**

- Keep steps and strength training
- Try to increase your lifts

**Plateaus:** read [why the scale stopped moving](/blog/weight-loss-plateau-why-scale-stopped) and [water weight vs fat](/blog/water-weight-vs-fat).

# A sample 1,600 kcal day

- **Breakfast:** 2 idlis or 1 ragi dosa with sambar, 2 boiled eggs
- **Lunch:** 1 cup rice, 150 g chicken, fish or paneer, poriyal, salad, curd
- **Evening:** buttermilk and sprouts or sundal
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal, vegetables

# How to track progress

- **Weekly average weight**
- **Waist** every week
- **Photos** every 30 days
- **Strength** in your log
- **How your clothes fit**

# After day 90

- **More to lose?** Continue, with another diet break every 8–12 weeks
- **Happy with your weight?** Slowly add 100–200 kcal a week until your weight is stable

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I lose 15 kg in 90 days?**
If you start heavy (110 kg or more), possibly. For most people, 6–10 kg is a great 90 days. See [can you lose 10 to 20 kg in 60 days](/blog/lose-10-to-20-kg-in-60-days).

**Do I need the gym?**
No, but strength training helps keep muscle. See [how to lose weight without a gym](/blog/lose-weight-without-gym).

**What if I miss a few days?**
Just continue. Ninety days of "mostly on track" beats a perfect week followed by giving up.
