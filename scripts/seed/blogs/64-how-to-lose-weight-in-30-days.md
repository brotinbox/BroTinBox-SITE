> **Quick answer:** In 30 days, most people can safely lose about **2–4 kg of body fat**, plus some water weight in the first week, by eating **about 500 kcal below maintenance**, getting **1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg**, walking **8,000–10,000 steps a day** and strength training **2–4 times a week**. Heavier people often lose faster at first. Aim for **0.5–1% of your body weight per week**.

Thirty days is a great time frame for a fresh start. It is long enough to see a clear change on the scale and in your clothes, and short enough to stay motivated. Here is a simple plan that works with Indian food.

# What to expect in 30 days

- **Week 1:** often 1–2 kg down. Much of this is water and less food in your gut.
- **Weeks 2–4:** about 0.5–1 kg a week, mostly fat.
- **Total:** around 3–5 kg on the scale for most people, more if you start heavier.

Fat loss is slower than water loss. Do not judge the plan by week 1 alone, and do not panic if week 3 is slower than week 2.

# Step 1: Set your calorie target

Estimate your maintenance calories:

- **Mostly sitting:** body weight (kg) × 25–28
- **Moderately active:** body weight (kg) × 29–32

Then subtract **about 500 kcal**.

Example: an 80 kg office worker. Maintenance is about 80 × 27 = 2,160 kcal. Target: **about 1,650 kcal a day**.

Do not go below about 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) without medical guidance. Our [calorie deficit guide](/blog/calorie-deficit-how-to-calculate) shows the full method.

# Step 2: Make protein the centre of every meal

High protein keeps you full and protects muscle while you lose fat. Aim for **1.2–1.6 g per kg** of body weight, or about **25–40 g per meal**.

Good options:

- Eggs, chicken, fish
- Paneer, tofu, curd
- Dal, chana, rajma, sprouts, soya chunks

# Step 3: Fix your plate

A simple Indian plate for weight loss:

- **Half the plate:** vegetables, salad, poriyal, kootu
- **A quarter:** protein (chicken, fish, eggs, paneer, dal)
- **A quarter:** carbs (1 cup rice or 2 chapatis)

You do not need to quit rice. Just measure it. Read [is rice bad for weight loss](/blog/is-rice-bad-for-weight-loss) and [how many chapatis for weight loss](/blog/how-many-chapati-for-weight-loss).

# Step 4: Cut the hidden calories

These are where most Indian diets go over:

- **Oil:** 1 tbsp is about 120 kcal. Measure it.
- **Sugar in tea and coffee:** 3 cups with 2 tsp sugar each is about 100 kcal a day.
- **Fried snacks:** bajji, vadai, samosa, mixture
- **Sweets and juices**
- **Late-night eating** out of boredom

# Step 5: Move every day

- **Walk 8,000–10,000 steps a day.** It burns more than most people think. See [walking for weight loss](/blog/walking-for-weight-loss-steps).
- **Strength train 2–4 times a week** to keep muscle, so the weight you lose is fat.
- Cardio is a bonus, not the main tool.

# A sample 1,600 kcal day

- **Breakfast:** 2 idlis with sambar and 2 boiled eggs
- **Lunch:** 1 cup rice, 150 g chicken or fish curry (less oil), poriyal, salad
- **Evening:** buttermilk and a cup of sundal
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal, vegetables, curd

About **1,600 kcal and 90–100 g protein**. Vegetarians can swap chicken for paneer, tofu or extra dal.

# Track the right things

- **Weigh 3–4 times a week**, same time, and look at the weekly average.
- **Measure your waist** once a week.
- **Take a photo** on day 1 and day 30.

If the scale has not moved for 2 weeks, read [why your weight loss stopped](/blog/weight-loss-plateau-why-scale-stopped).

# What not to do

- **Crash diets** under 1,000 kcal. You lose muscle, stall and regain fast.
- **Juice-only or detox plans.**
- **Skipping meals,** then overeating at night.
- **Expecting 10 kg in 30 days.** For most people that is not fat loss, and it does not last.

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I lose belly fat in 30 days?**
You cannot target belly fat alone, but your waist usually shrinks as overall fat goes down. Read [how to lose belly fat](/blog/how-to-lose-belly-fat-what-works).

**Do I need to go to the gym?**
No. Diet and walking can do most of the work. Strength training helps you keep muscle.

**What after 30 days?**
Keep going at the same pace, or take a week at maintenance and then continue.
