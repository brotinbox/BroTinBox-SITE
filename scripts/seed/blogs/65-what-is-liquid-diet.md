> **Quick answer:** A **liquid diet** means getting most or all of your calories from **drinks instead of solid food**: shakes, soups, juices, milk or medical formulas. There are four main types: **clear liquid** and **full liquid** diets (used short-term in hospitals, for example before or after surgery), **meal-replacement shake** plans for weight loss, and **juice cleanses**. Medical liquid diets are useful for specific reasons. For everyday weight loss, they are hard to sustain and often lead to weight regain.

Liquid diets are popular on social media as a quick way to "reset" or lose weight. But the term covers very different things, from a hospital diet after surgery to a 3-day juice cleanse. Here is what each one means.

# The 4 main types of liquid diet

## 1. Clear liquid diet

Only see-through liquids:

- Water, plain tea, black coffee
- Clear soups and broths (rasam without dal, vegetable or chicken stock)
- Coconut water, clear juices without pulp
- ORS

**Used for:** a day or two before some medical tests (like a colonoscopy) or right after surgery or a stomach illness.

**Not for weight loss.** It has almost no protein and very few nutrients. It is meant for 1–3 days, under medical advice.

## 2. Full liquid diet

Everything in a clear diet, plus thicker liquids:

- Milk, buttermilk, lassi, smooth curd
- Cream soups, smooth dal water, thin porridge (kanji, ragi koozh)
- Smoothies and milkshakes
- Protein shakes

**Used for:** recovery after mouth, jaw or stomach surgery, or when chewing and swallowing are difficult. Usually for days to a couple of weeks.

## 3. Meal-replacement shake diets

One or two meals a day are replaced with a measured shake that has protein, carbs, fats, vitamins and minerals. The other meal is normal food.

**Used for:** weight loss. Some medically supervised programmes use very low calorie shake diets (around 800 kcal a day) for people with obesity or type 2 diabetes, for a limited time, with doctor monitoring.

## 4. Juice cleanses and "detox" diets

Only fruit and vegetable juices for 1–7 days.

**Claimed for:** detox and quick weight loss.

**Reality:** your liver and kidneys already detox your body. Juices are low in protein and often high in sugar. The weight lost is mostly water and comes back quickly.

# What a liquid diet can be good for

- **Medical reasons:** before tests, after surgery, during some illnesses
- **Short-term structure:** a shake for breakfast can help some busy people control calories
- **Supervised weight loss:** for people with obesity, under a doctor or dietitian

# The downsides

- **Low protein:** most juices and soups have little, which means muscle loss
- **Hunger:** liquids fill you up less than solid food, so cravings build up
- **Low fibre:** can cause constipation
- **Rapid weight loss risks:** very fast loss increases the risk of **gallstones**
- **Weight regain:** once you go back to normal food, the weight usually returns
- **Social life:** hard to keep up with family meals and work lunches

# Who should not do a liquid diet on their own

- People with **diabetes**, especially on insulin or tablets (risk of low sugar)
- People with **kidney, liver or heart disease**
- **Pregnant or breastfeeding** women
- Anyone with a history of **eating disorders**
- Teenagers

If a doctor has put you on a liquid diet, follow their plan exactly.

# A better way to use liquids

You do not need to drink all your meals. Instead:

- Start lunch with a bowl of **vegetable soup or rasam**. It fills you up for few calories.
- Swap sugary drinks for **buttermilk or lemon water** without sugar.
- Use a **protein shake** (with or without powder) when you miss a meal. See [protein shakes without protein powder](/blog/protein-shake-without-protein-powder).

This gives you the benefits without the crash.

# Frequently asked questions

**How long can you stay on a liquid diet?**
Clear liquid diets: usually 1–3 days. Full liquid diets: days to a couple of weeks. Meal-replacement plans: weeks to months, ideally with professional guidance.

**Is a liquid diet good for weight loss?**
It can cause fast weight loss, but much of it is water and muscle, and most people regain it. A measured calorie deficit with real food lasts better.

**Is soup a liquid diet?**
Clear soups fit a clear liquid diet. Thick soups fit a full liquid diet.
