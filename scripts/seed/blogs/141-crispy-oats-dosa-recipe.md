Oats dosa gives you the crisp, golden dosa you love with more fibre than a plain rice dosa. This is a home-style version of the **Crispy Oats Dosa** in our BroTin Box menu, made from fermented oats and lentil batter.

# Fermented oats dosa (best taste)

## Ingredients (makes 12–14 dosas)

- 1 cup rolled oats
- ½ cup raw rice or idli rice
- ¼ cup urad dal
- 1 tbsp chana dal
- ½ tsp methi (fenugreek) seeds
- Salt to taste
- Water for grinding
- Oil for the tawa (about ¼ tsp per dosa)

## Method

1. **Soak:** wash rice, urad dal, chana dal and methi; soak together for **4–5 hours**. Soak the oats separately for **20 minutes**.
2. **Grind:** grind everything to a **smooth batter**, adding water gradually.
3. **Ferment:** mix in salt, cover and leave in a warm place for **8–12 hours** until slightly risen and bubbly.
4. **Adjust:** stir; the batter should be pourable but not watery.
5. **Heat the tawa:** medium-high, then sprinkle water; it should sizzle. Wipe with a cloth dipped in a little oil.
6. **Spread:** pour a ladle of batter in the centre and **spread thin in circles**. Drizzle a few drops of oil around the edge.
7. **Cook:** until the bottom is **golden and crisp**, about 1.5–2 minutes. Fold and serve; no need to flip thin dosas.

# Instant oats dosa (no fermentation)

## Ingredients (makes 6–8)

- 1 cup rolled oats, powdered in a mixer
- ¼ cup rice flour
- ¼ cup fine rava (sooji)
- ¼ cup curd
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, chopped
- A few curry leaves and coriander, chopped
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- Salt to taste
- About 1½–2 cups water

## Method

1. Mix all dry ingredients, curd and water into a **thin, flowing batter** (like rava dosa).
2. Rest for **15–20 minutes**; it will thicken, so add a little water.
3. On a hot tawa, **pour the batter from a height** to make a lacy dosa. Fill large gaps lightly.
4. Drizzle a few drops of oil. Cook until **crisp and golden**, about 2–3 minutes.

**Note:** instant oats dosa contains rava, which is wheat.

# Nutrition

For **two thin dosas (about 70 g)** like our kitchen's:

- **Calories:** about 185 kcal
- **Protein:** about 5.5 g
- **Carbs:** about 31 g
- **Fibre:** about 4.5 g
- **Fat:** about 4 g

# Make it a high-protein breakfast

Dosa alone is mostly carbs. Add:

- **2 boiled eggs or an egg podi topping:** +12 g protein
- **Sambar (1 cup):** +4–5 g
- **A cup of sundal:** +8–13 g. See [black chana sundal](/blog/black-chana-sundal-recipe).
- **Paneer bhurji filling:** +10–15 g

**Example:** 2 oats dosas + sambar + 2 boiled eggs = about 400 kcal and 22 g protein.

# Tips for crisp oats dosa

- **Hot tawa, thin spread:** thick dosas turn soft
- **Season your tawa** with a halved onion dipped in oil
- **Do not overcrowd the batter with vegetables** if you want it crisp
- **Store** fermented batter in the fridge for 2–3 days

# Frequently asked questions

**Is oats dosa good for weight loss?**
Yes, especially with a protein side. It has more fibre than a plain rice dosa.

**Is oats dosa gluten-free?**
Not reliably. Oats are often processed with wheat, and the instant version uses rava. Use certified gluten-free oats and skip rava if needed.

**Can diabetics eat oats dosa?**
It is usually a better option than plain rice dosa; pair it with protein and follow your doctor's advice on portions.
