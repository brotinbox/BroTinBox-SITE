# What makes a healthy office lunch?

- **25–40 g of protein,** so you stay full and focused all afternoon
- **Measured carbs,** not 3 cups of rice
- **Vegetables or salad**
- **Low oil**
- **Delivered on time,** so you do not end up ordering fried food at 2 PM
