> **Quick answer:** Losing **5 kg in 60 days is realistic and safe** for most people with extra weight, at about **0.5–0.75 kg a week**. Losing **8–10 kg in 60 days** is possible if you start **heavier (around 90–100 kg or more)**, as you can lose about 1 kg a week safely. The plan: eat **500–750 kcal below maintenance**, get **1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg**, walk **8,000–10,000 steps a day** and strength train **2–4 times a week**.

Sixty days is a great window for weight loss. Unlike 30-day plans, it gives you time to lose real fat, not just water. Here is how much you can expect and a plan to get there.

# How much can you lose in 60 days?

A safe rate is about **0.5–1% of your body weight a week**.

- **70 kg:** about 0.35–0.7 kg a week, so **3–5 kg** in 60 days
- **85 kg:** about 0.45–0.85 kg a week, so **4–7 kg**
- **100 kg:** about 0.5–1 kg a week, so **5–9 kg**
- **120 kg:** about 0.6–1.2 kg a week, so **6–10 kg**

Add 1–2 kg of water loss in the first week or two. So **5 kg** is a realistic goal for most people, and **10 kg** is realistic only if you are starting heavy.

# The 60-day plan

## Phase 1 (days 1–14): set the habits

- **Calories:** maintenance minus 500 kcal. See [how to calculate a calorie deficit](/blog/calorie-deficit-how-to-calculate).
- **Protein:** 1.2–1.6 g per kg, about 25–40 g per meal.
- **Steps:** build up to 8,000 a day.
- **Training:** 2–3 strength sessions a week.
- **Cut:** sugar in tea, juices, fried snacks and late-night eating.

Expect 1.5–3 kg down, partly water.

## Phase 2 (days 15–45): steady fat loss

- **Steps:** 8,000–10,000 a day.
- **Training:** 3–4 sessions a week.
- **Weigh-ins:** 3–4 times a week; track the weekly average.
- **If the average stalls for 2 weeks:** cut 150–200 kcal or add 2,000 steps a day.

Expect 0.5–1 kg a week.

## Phase 3 (days 46–60): stay consistent

This is where many people quit, because loss slows a little. Keep going:

- Keep protein high
- Plan for weekends and functions; see [cheat day or cheat meal](/blog/cheat-day)
- Sleep 7–8 hours

If you hit a plateau, read [why the scale stopped moving](/blog/weight-loss-plateau-why-scale-stopped).

# What to eat: a simple Indian plate

- **Half the plate:** vegetables, salad, poriyal, keerai
- **A quarter:** protein (eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, tofu, dal, chana)
- **A quarter:** 1 cup rice or 2 chapatis or 2–3 idlis

**Sample 1,600 kcal day**

- **Breakfast:** 2 boiled eggs and 2 idlis with sambar
- **Lunch:** 150 g grilled chicken or paneer, 1 cup rice, poriyal, salad, curd
- **Evening:** buttermilk and sprouts
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal, vegetables

# Exercise that helps most

- **Walking:** the easiest daily calorie burner. See [walking for weight loss](/blog/walking-for-weight-loss-steps).
- **Strength training:** keeps your muscle so the weight you lose is fat.
- **Cardio:** a bonus; try [zone 2 cardio](/blog/zone-2-cardio) or the [12-3-30 treadmill workout](/blog/12-3-30-treadmill-workout).

# How to measure progress

- **Weight:** weekly average, not daily numbers
- **Waist:** at the navel, every week
- **Photos:** day 1, day 30, day 60
- **Clothes:** often the first thing you notice

# Signs you are going too fast

- Dizziness, constant tiredness
- Hair fall
- Missed periods
- Strength dropping fast
- Binge eating

If you notice these, add 200–300 kcal and slow down.

# Frequently asked questions

**Is 10 kg in 60 days safe?**
For people starting at 100 kg or more, it can be. For most people around 70 kg, it would be too fast.

**Do I need to stop eating rice?**
No. Measure it: about 1 cup cooked per meal. See [is rice bad for weight loss](/blog/is-rice-bad-for-weight-loss).

**What should I do after 60 days?**
Continue if you have more to lose, or spend 2–4 weeks at maintenance to settle.
