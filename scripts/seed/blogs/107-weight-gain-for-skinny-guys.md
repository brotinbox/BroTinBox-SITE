> **Quick answer:** Most skinny guys do not have a "fast metabolism problem". They simply **eat less than they think**, often skip meals, and fill up on low-calorie food. To gain weight: eat about **400–600 kcal above maintenance**, get **1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg**, eat **4–5 times a day**, add **calorie-dense foods** (milk, peanuts, peanut butter, bananas, ghee in moderation, paneer, rice), and **lift weights 3–5 days a week**. Aim for **0.25–0.5 kg a week**.

"I eat a lot but I never gain." If that sounds like you, you are not alone. Here is why skinny guys struggle to gain, and an Indian diet plan that works.

# Why you are not gaining

## 1. You eat less than you think

Many skinny people eat one big meal and then barely eat for hours. When they track for a week, they often find they eat **1,600–1,900 kcal**, not the 3,000 they imagined.

## 2. Low appetite

You feel full quickly and are not hungry for long.

## 3. You fill up on "light" food

Lots of vegetables, salads, fruit, tea and water fill your stomach without many calories.

## 4. Your daily movement is high

Walking a lot, fidgeting, playing sports or a physical job can burn more than you realise.

## 5. You do not train

Without strength training, extra food does not build muscle; you look the same, just a little softer.

# Step 1: Track for 3 days

Write down everything you eat for 3 normal days. Most skinny guys are surprised by how low the number is. Then add **400–600 kcal** to that.

Or estimate: **body weight (kg) × 35–40** for a skinny, active person.

# Step 2: Eat more often

- **4–5 meals a day,** every 3–4 hours
- **Set reminders** on your phone if you forget to eat
- **Never skip breakfast**

# Step 3: Use calorie-dense foods

These add calories without making you feel stuffed:

- **Milk:** 1 glass = about 150 kcal
- **Peanuts and peanut butter:** 2 tbsp = about 190 kcal. See [peanut butter for muscle gain](/blog/peanut-butter-for-muscle-gain).
- **Bananas and dates**
- **Paneer:** 100 g = about 260–320 kcal
- **Curd rice, ghee rice** (ghee in moderation)
- **Dry fruits:** raisins, dates, cashews
- **Oats cooked in milk**

# Step 4: Drink some calories

Liquids fill you up less than solids. A homemade shake can add **500–700 kcal** easily:

- 300 ml milk + 1 banana + 2 tbsp peanut butter + 3 tbsp oats + 2 dates

See [protein shakes without protein powder](/blog/protein-shake-without-protein-powder).

# Step 5: Lift weights

- **3–5 days a week**
- Focus on **squats, bench press, rows, overhead press, deadlifts**
- **Add weight or reps every week**

Follow our [gym workout plan for beginners](/blog/gym-workout-plan-for-beginners) or [push pull legs plan](/blog/push-pull-legs-workout-plan).

# A 2,800 kcal day for a skinny guy (60 kg)

- **7 AM:** 3-egg omelette, 2 chapatis with peanut butter, a glass of milk
- **10:30 AM:** the big shake above
- **1 PM:** 150 g chicken or paneer, 1.5–2 cups rice, dal, curd
- **4:30 PM:** 2 bananas and a handful of peanuts
- **7 PM (after gym):** a glass of milk
- **8:30 PM:** fish or soya curry, 3 chapatis, vegetables
- **10 PM:** curd with dates

About **2,800 kcal and 120–130 g protein**.

# How fast should you gain?

- **0.25–0.5 kg a week** for mostly lean gain
- Faster is possible if you are underweight, but more of it will be fat

See [how to gain 5 to 10 kg in 60 days](/blog/gain-5-to-10-kg-in-60-days).

# Avoid these traps

- **Junk food every day:** it fills you with fat, not muscle
- **"Weight gainer" tablets or ayurvedic powders** with unknown ingredients; some contain hidden steroids
- **Giving up after 2 weeks** because the mirror has not changed
- **Doing only cardio**

# When to see a doctor

If you eat well and still lose weight, or you have diarrhoea, stomach pain, excessive thirst or fatigue, see a doctor. Thyroid problems, diabetes, worms or digestive issues can stop weight gain.

# Frequently asked questions

**Is it a fast metabolism?**
Usually not. Most skinny people simply do not eat enough consistently.

**Do I need mass gainer?**
No. Milk, peanuts, bananas and oats in a homemade shake work just as well.

**Can I gain weight on a veg diet?**
Yes. Milk, paneer, curd, soya, peanuts, dal and rice provide plenty.
