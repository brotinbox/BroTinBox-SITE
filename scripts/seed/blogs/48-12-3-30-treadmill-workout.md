> **Quick answer:** The **12-3-30 workout** means walking on a treadmill at a **12% incline**, at **3 miles per hour (about 4.8 km/h)**, for **30 minutes**. For a 70 kg person, it burns roughly **250–350 kcal** per session. It is a good, low-impact cardio workout that works the glutes, hamstrings and calves, but it is not magic. Fat loss still depends on your overall calorie deficit.

The 12-3-30 went viral after a social media creator shared it as the routine that helped her lose weight. Since then, it has become one of the most searched treadmill workouts in the world. Does it actually work?

# What is 12-3-30?

Set your treadmill to:

- **Incline:** 12%
- **Speed:** 3 mph (about **4.8 km/h** on Indian treadmills, which usually show km/h)
- **Time:** 30 minutes

Walk without holding the handrails, at a steady pace.

# How many calories does it burn?

It depends on your body weight. Rough estimates for 30 minutes:

- **55 kg:** about 200–260 kcal
- **70 kg:** about 250–350 kcal
- **85 kg:** about 300–400 kcal

Walking uphill burns much more than walking on flat ground at the same speed. A flat 30-minute walk at 4.8 km/h burns roughly 120–150 kcal for a 70 kg person, so the incline more than doubles it.

Note: treadmill calorie counters often overestimate. Treat the displayed number as a rough guide.

# Why people like it

- **Low impact.** Easier on knees and ankles than running.
- **Simple.** No intervals or complicated settings.
- **Works the backside.** Incline walking strongly works glutes and hamstrings.
- **Good for beginners** and people with a lot of weight to lose who find running hard.
- **Steady heart rate.** It often lands in a "zone 2" style effort. More on that in our upcoming zone 2 cardio guide.

# Is 12-3-30 good for weight loss?

It can help, as part of a plan:

- **300 kcal a day, 5 days a week** adds up to about 1,500 kcal a week, about 0.2 kg of fat if your eating stays the same.
- Combined with a calorie deficit from your diet, it can speed up fat loss.

But remember:

- You can easily **eat back** 300 kcal with one samosa and a sweet tea.
- Cardio alone, without strength training, can lead to muscle loss when dieting.

Fat loss is mostly about your diet. Use our [calorie deficit guide](/blog/calorie-deficit-how-to-calculate) to set your numbers.

# If 12-3-30 is too hard at first

Most beginners cannot do the full 30 minutes at 12% on day one. That is normal. Build up:

- **Week 1:** 6–8% incline, 4.5 km/h, 15–20 minutes
- **Week 2:** 8–10% incline, 4.8 km/h, 20–25 minutes
- **Week 3:** 10–12% incline, 4.8 km/h, 25–30 minutes
- **Week 4:** full 12-3-30

# Tips to do it safely

- **Do not hold the handrails.** It reduces the effort and strains your posture.
- **Stand tall**, with a slight forward lean from your ankles.
- **Wear proper shoes.**
- **Warm up for 3–5 minutes** at a lower incline.
- **Drink water.** Incline walking makes you sweat a lot.
- If you have **knee, back or heart problems**, check with a doctor or physiotherapist first.

# 12-3-30 vs walking outdoors

No treadmill? Walking outdoors works too:

- Find a slope or climb stairs for part of your walk.
- A brisk 45-minute walk on flat ground gives a similar burn to 12-3-30.
- Morning walks at Race Course or along the Ukkadam lake walkways are great in Coimbatore's cooler months.

# How often should you do it?

- **3–5 times a week** is enough for most people.
- Keep **2–3 days of strength training** in your week. Read our [beginner gym workout plan](/blog/gym-workout-plan-for-beginners).
- Do not do it right before a heavy leg session.

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I do 12-3-30 every day?**
You can, but your calves and shins may need rest. Most people do best with 3–5 days a week.

**Is 12-3-30 better than running?**
For burning calories per minute, running usually burns more. 12-3-30 is easier on the joints and easier to stick with.

**Will 12-3-30 reduce belly fat?**
It helps overall fat loss. Belly fat comes off as total body fat drops. See [how to lose belly fat](/blog/how-to-lose-belly-fat-what-works).

**Should I do it fasted?**
It does not matter much for fat loss. Do it at the time you feel best.
