> **Quick answer:** A college student can eat **100+ g of protein a day on a budget** using **eggs, soya chunks, milk, curd, peanuts, chana and dal**, the cheapest protein sources per gram in India. Add rice or chapati, bananas and vegetables for energy. You do not need protein powder, chicken every day or expensive "diet food" to build muscle in college.

Coimbatore is a college city. Thousands of students living in hostels, PGs and shared rooms want to get fit, but they are working with mess food, a small budget and no proper kitchen.

Here is how to eat for the gym as a student without spending a lot.

# The cheapest protein sources

Roughly ranked by protein per rupee (prices vary by shop and season):

1. **Soya chunks:** about 52 g protein per 100 g dry. Usually the cheapest protein per gram.
2. **Eggs:** about 6 g protein each. Cheap, quick, no fridge needed for a day or two.
3. **Peanuts:** about 25 g protein per 100 g. Great snack.
4. **Chana (black or white), green gram, rajma:** about 20 g protein per 100 g dry.
5. **Milk and curd:** about 3–4 g protein per 100 ml or 100 g.
6. **Dal:** about 22–25 g protein per 100 g dry.
7. **Chicken:** about 22–23 g per 100 g raw breast. More expensive, but good once or twice a week.
8. **Paneer:** about 18–20 g per 100 g. Tasty but costlier.

See [soya chunks protein](/blog/soya-chunks-protein), [protein in egg](/blog/protein-in-egg) and [protein in dal, chana and rajma](/blog/protein-in-dal-chana-rajma) for detailed numbers.

# A budget gym day for a 60 kg student (about 100 g protein)

**Breakfast (hostel mess or room)**
- 3 boiled eggs, about 18 g
- Mess idli, dosa or pongal with sambar, about 6 g
- 1 banana

**Lunch (mess)**
- Rice, sambar, poriyal
- Extra dal or curd if available, about 10 g
- 2 more boiled eggs (carry them), about 12 g

**Evening (before gym)**
- Boiled peanuts or roasted chana (about 50 g), about 10–12 g
- 1 banana

**Post-gym / dinner**
- Soya chunks (40 g dry), cooked in an electric kettle or rice cooker, or ordered from a nearby mess, about 20 g
- Chapati or rice
- 1 glass of milk, about 8 g

**Total: about 95–105 g protein.**

# No kitchen? Here is what still works

Many hostels do not allow cooking. Options:

- **Boiled eggs:** buy ready-boiled eggs from nearby shops, or use an electric egg boiler if allowed.
- **Roasted chana and peanuts:** no cooking at all.
- **Milk:** packets from the nearest store.
- **Curd:** cups from the store.
- **Sattu:** mix with water or milk for a quick protein drink.
- **Sundal from local shops** in the evening.

# Making mess food work

- **Eat the dal, sambar, kootu and curd** first. They carry the protein.
- **Take a little less rice** and more vegetables.
- **Skip the fried items** most days (bajji, vadai, appalam).
- **Add your own eggs** to lunch and dinner.

# Do you need protein powder as a student?

No. Whey is convenient, but eggs, soya, milk and peanuts can easily take you to your target for much less. If you buy whey, buy only from trusted sellers; fake supplements are common. Read [is whey protein enough to build muscle](/blog/is-whey-protein-enough-to-build-muscle).

# Training on a student schedule

- **3–4 gym days a week** is enough. Use our [beginner gym workout plan](/blog/gym-workout-plan-for-beginners).
- **Walk** to college or around campus.
- **Sleep 7–8 hours.** Late-night scrolling kills gains.
- Many college gyms and local gyms in Coimbatore offer student rates. Ask.

# Budget tips

1. **Buy eggs by the tray** instead of a few at a time.
2. **Buy soya chunks, chana and peanuts in 1 kg packs.**
3. **Share bulk buys** with roommates.
4. **Avoid paying for "diet" snacks** like protein bars, which are expensive per gram of protein.
5. **Cut cool drinks and packaged snacks first.** That money buys a lot of eggs.

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I build muscle on hostel food?**
Yes, if you add cheap protein like eggs, soya, peanuts and milk to the mess food, and train consistently.

**How many eggs should a student eat a day?**
3–5 eggs a day is common among students who train. Read [how many eggs a day is safe](/blog/how-many-eggs-a-day-is-safe).

**Is soya safe for young men?**
Yes. In normal amounts (25–50 g dry a day), research does not show effects on testosterone.

**What if I am vegetarian?**
Focus on soya, paneer, curd, milk, dal, chana and peanuts. 80–100 g of protein a day is very achievable.
