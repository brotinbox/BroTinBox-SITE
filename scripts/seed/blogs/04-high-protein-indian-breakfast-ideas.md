> **Quick answer:** The easiest high-protein Indian breakfasts are egg bhurji with chapati (about 20–25 g protein), paneer bhurji (about 20 g), moong dal chilla or pesarattu with curd (about 15–20 g), soya upma (about 20 g), and oats pongal with a side of boiled eggs (about 20 g). Aim for **20–30 g of protein at breakfast** to stay full until lunch.

Most Indian breakfasts are delicious and carbohydrate-heavy. Two dosas, three idlis or a plate of upma usually deliver only **5–10 g of protein**. That is why many people feel hungry by 11 AM and reach for biscuits, tea and snacks.

Adding protein to breakfast is one of the simplest changes you can make. It keeps you fuller for longer, helps steady your energy through the morning, and makes it far easier to hit your daily protein target. Not sure what your target is? Read [how much protein you need per day](/blog/how-much-protein-per-day).

# Egg-based breakfasts

## 1. Egg bhurji with 2 chapatis (about 22–25 g protein)
Three eggs scrambled with onion, tomato, green chilli and turmeric. Quick, filling, and easy to make in under 10 minutes.

## 2. Egg dosa (about 12–14 g per dosa)
Crack an egg onto your dosa as it cooks. Two egg dosas give you a solid protein base with a familiar taste.

## 3. Boiled eggs with idli and sambar (about 18–20 g)
Add 2–3 boiled eggs to your usual 3 idlis. The simplest upgrade there is.

## 4. Masala omelette with toast or chapati (about 18–20 g)
Three-egg omelette loaded with onion, capsicum and coriander.

# Vegetarian breakfasts

## 5. Paneer bhurji with chapati (about 20 g)
100 g of paneer crumbled and cooked like egg bhurji. Rich and very filling.

## 6. Pesarattu with curd (about 15–18 g)
Andhra-style green gram dosa. Whole moong is naturally high in protein and fibre. Serve with a bowl of curd instead of only chutney.

## 7. Moong dal chilla with hung curd dip (about 15–18 g)
Soaked and ground yellow moong made into thin pancakes. Pair with thick hung curd for extra protein.

## 8. Soya upma or soya poha (about 18–22 g)
Add 30–40 g of soaked soya granules to your usual upma or poha. You will barely taste it, and the protein jumps.

## 9. Sprouted green gram salad (about 12–14 g)
Sprouted pachai payaru with onion, tomato, lemon and a pinch of pepper. A cooling, fibre-rich side for any breakfast.

## 10. Kollu (horse gram) sundal (about 10–12 g)
A traditional Kongu favourite. Pair it with ragi dosa for a filling, low-oil breakfast.

## 11. Oats pongal with moong dal (about 10–12 g, more with a side)
Swap rice for oats and keep the moong dal. Add 2 boiled eggs or a cup of curd to take it past 20 g.

# Quick no-cook options

## 13. Greek yoghurt or hung curd bowl (about 15–20 g)
200 g of hung curd with fruit and a few nuts.

## 14. Milk with roasted chana and peanuts (about 15 g)
One glass of milk, a handful of roasted chana and a small handful of peanuts. Ideal when you are rushing out of the door.

## 15. Peanut butter on whole wheat toast with milk (about 15–18 g)
Choose peanut butter with no added sugar.

# How to build a high-protein breakfast plate

Use this simple formula:

1. **Start with a protein anchor:** eggs, paneer, soya, sprouts or legumes (aim for 15–25 g).
2. **Add your favourite carb:** idli, dosa, chapati, oats or poha.
3. **Add a side that also has protein:** curd, milk or sundal instead of only chutney.
4. **Keep oil measured:** a teaspoon, not a ladle.

# Frequently asked questions

**How much protein should breakfast have?**
For most adults, 20–30 g is a good target. If you train hard or weigh over 80 kg, 30–40 g is reasonable.

**Is it bad to eat eggs every day?**
For most healthy people, eating 1–3 eggs a day is fine as part of a balanced diet. If you have high cholesterol or diabetes, ask your doctor about the right amount for you.

**Can I get a high-protein breakfast without eggs?**
Yes. Paneer, soya, pesarattu, moong chilla, sprouts and hung curd all work well. Ideas 5 to 15 above are egg-free.

**Is a protein shake a good breakfast?**
It is convenient, but whole food keeps you fuller and gives you fibre and micronutrients. Use a shake as a backup, not the default.
