# What a good weight loss meal plan needs

- **A calorie deficit:** about 500 kcal below your maintenance
- **High protein:** 25–40 g per main meal to stay full and protect muscle
- **Fibre:** vegetables and salad at every meal
- **Measured carbs:** rice and chapati in sensible portions, not cut out
- **Low oil:** 1 tbsp of oil is about 120 kcal
- **Something you can follow for months,** not a 7-day crash plan

# Option 2: A home-cooked weight loss plan

If you cook, use the **plate method**:

- **Half the plate:** vegetables, keerai, poriyal, salad
- **A quarter:** protein (chicken, fish, eggs, paneer, dal)
- **A quarter:** 1 cup rice or 2 chapatis

**A sample Coimbatore day (about 1,600 kcal)**

- **Breakfast:** ragi dosa (2) with chutney and 2 boiled eggs
- **Lunch:** 1 cup rice, 150 g chicken or fish curry (less oil), poriyal, rasam, curd
- **Evening:** tea without sugar and roasted chana
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal, vegetable kurma (light)
