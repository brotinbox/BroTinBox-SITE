> **Quick answer:** A good office lunch has about 30 g of protein, a measured portion of rice or chapati, and plenty of vegetables. Grilled chicken, eggs, paneer, tofu, soya chunks and sprouts all make this easy. Keep your tiffin refrigerated until lunch if it will sit for more than 2 hours, and take a short walk afterwards.

It is 3 PM. The meeting is still going, your eyes are heavy, and you are thinking about tea and something fried. For many desk workers, that slump is partly a lunch problem.

A large plate of refined carbohydrates with little protein can leave you sleepy and hungry again quickly. A better-balanced lunch will not fix a bad night's sleep, but it can make your afternoon a lot steadier.

# The office lunch formula

Build your tiffin from four parts:

- **Protein, about 30 g:** the anchor of the meal
- **Carbs, about 1 cup rice or 2 chapatis:** enough to keep you going, without the heaviness
- **Vegetables or salad:** for fibre and volume
- **Flavour:** pickle in small amounts, chutney, podi or lemon, so that you actually look forward to it

# How much protein is in common tiffin foods?

These are approximate:

- **Grilled chicken breast, 100 g cooked:** about 31 g
- **2 boiled eggs:** about 12.5 g
- **Paneer, 100 g:** about 18 g
- **Firm tofu, 150 g:** about 18 g
- **Soya chunks, 30 g dry:** about 15 g
- **Sprouted green gram, 100 g:** about 8.5 g
- **Thick curd, 1 cup:** about 8–10 g

Compare them in detail in our [chicken vs eggs vs paneer vs soya guide](/blog/chicken-eggs-paneer-soya-protein-comparison).

# 8 tiffin ideas with 25 g of protein or more

1. **Chicken and chapati:** 100 g grilled chicken, 2 chapatis and cabbage salad
2. **Egg curry rice:** 3 eggs in a light curry, 1 cup rice and cucumber
3. **Paneer bhurji wrap:** 100 g paneer in 2 chapatis, with a small cup of curd
4. **Soya chunk pulao:** 40 g dry soya chunks cooked with 1 cup of rice and vegetables
5. **Fish and rice:** 100–120 g fish fry made with measured oil, 1 cup rice and poriyal
6. **Tofu and millet bowl:** 150 g tofu, a cup of cooked millet, and stir-fried vegetables
7. **Rajma and egg bowl:** a cup of rajma, 2 boiled eggs and salad
8. **Chana and curd rice:** a cup of black chana sundal, with a smaller curd rice

Most vegetarian options need two protein sources, for example paneer with curd, or soya with sprouts. That is normal, so plan for it.

# Keep your lunch safe

This matters more than most people realise, especially in a warm city. Food left at room temperature for hours gives bacteria time to grow.

- **Refrigerate your tiffin** at the office if lunch is more than 2 hours away.
- **No fridge?** Use an insulated bag with a cold pack, and keep curd and salads cold.
- **Reheat until piping hot** all the way through, and reheat only once.
- **Do not reheat curd or salad.** Pack them separately.

# Three habits that beat the slump

## 1. Do not skip breakfast and overeat at lunch

Skipping breakfast is fine for some people, but if it means a giant lunch, the afternoon will be harder. A protein breakfast can help. Here are [15 high-protein Indian breakfast ideas](/blog/high-protein-indian-breakfast-ideas).

## 2. Walk for 10 minutes after eating

Even a short walk after a meal can help with blood sugar control, and it is a good break from the screen.

## 3. Drink water, and watch the afternoon tea

Mild thirst can feel like tiredness. Two sugary teas and biscuits every afternoon can also add a few hundred calories that you do not notice.

# Eating from the canteen or ordering in?

If you do not carry a tiffin:

- Choose grilled or tandoori items over deep-fried options.
- Ask for an extra egg, or add a side of curd.
- Take less rice in the first serving, and do not go back for refills out of habit.
- Skip the fried appalam on most days.

# Frequently asked questions

**How much protein should I eat at lunch?**
Around 25–35 g works well for most adults. Your total for the day matters most, so use our [daily protein guide](/blog/how-much-protein-per-day) to find your number.

**Is curd rice a good office lunch?**
It is comforting, but it is low in protein for its calories. Add eggs, sundal or grilled chicken, or have a smaller portion alongside another protein.

**Why do I still feel sleepy after a healthy lunch?**
A dip in alertness after lunch is partly natural, and poor sleep makes it worse. If you feel extremely tired after meals on a regular basis, speak to your doctor.
