"Balanced diet food" usually means food that is not just healthy-sounding, but has the **right mix of protein, carbs, fats and fibre** in a **sensible calorie range**. Here is how to get that in Coimbatore, delivered or home-cooked.

# What a balanced meal looks like

- **Protein:** 25–40 g (chicken, fish, eggs, paneer, tofu, soya, dal)
- **Fibre:** vegetables, salad, legumes
- **Measured carbs:** rice, chapati, millets or oats in a set portion
- **Healthy fats:** a little oil, nuts or fish, not a pool of oil
- **Calories that fit your goal:** around 400–500 kcal for weight loss, 600–900 kcal for active people

Read [what is a balanced diet](/blog/what-is-a-balanced-diet) for the full explanation.

# Why most meals are not balanced

A typical Coimbatore lunch out:

- **Meals plate:** lots of rice, little protein
- **Biryani:** high calories, moderate protein, very little fibre
- **Parotta and salna:** high in refined flour and oil
- **Salads alone:** often too low in protein and calories to keep you full

# Balanced meals at home

**The balanced Indian plate**

- **Half:** vegetables and salad (poriyal, kootu, keerai, cucumber, carrot)
- **A quarter:** protein (dal, chana, eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, tofu)
- **A quarter:** 1 cup rice or 2 chapatis or 2–3 idlis
- **Side:** curd or buttermilk
- **Oil:** 1–2 tsp per person per meal

**A balanced Coimbatore day (about 1,700 kcal, 90 g protein)**

- **Breakfast:** 2 ragi dosas with sambar and 2 boiled eggs
- **Lunch:** 1 cup rice, fish or chicken curry, poriyal, rasam, curd
- **Evening:** buttermilk and [black chana sundal](/blog/black-chana-sundal-recipe)
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal, vegetables, salad
