> **Quick answer:** The **keto diet** cuts carbs to about **20–50 g a day** so your body burns fat and makes ketones. It can cause **fast early weight loss**, mostly water at first, and reduces appetite for some people. For Indians it is **hard to follow** because it removes rice, chapati, idli, dosa, most dal, most fruit and sugar. Long-term, it usually gives **similar weight loss to other diets** with the same calories. People with **diabetes on medicines (especially SGLT2 inhibitors), kidney or liver disease, or who are pregnant** should not try keto without a doctor.

Keto is popular in India, especially among people who want fast results. But how does it work with Indian food, and is it worth it? Here is an honest look.

# How keto works

Normally your body runs mainly on glucose from carbs. When carbs drop very low for a few days:

- Your stored glycogen (and the water it holds) is used up
- Your liver starts turning fat into **ketones**
- Your body and brain use ketones for fuel

This state is called **ketosis**.

# What you eat on keto

**Typical split:** about 70–75% calories from fat, 20% from protein, 5–10% from carbs.

**Allowed (Indian keto foods)**

- Eggs, chicken, fish, mutton
- Paneer, cheese, butter, ghee, cream
- Coconut, coconut oil
- Nuts and seeds in moderation (almonds, walnuts, flaxseed)
- Low-carb vegetables: spinach and other keerai, cabbage, cauliflower, capsicum, beans, cucumber, broccoli, mushrooms

**Not allowed (or very limited)**

- Rice, chapati, idli, dosa, upma, poha, millets
- Most dal, chana and rajma (too high in carbs)
- Potatoes, sweet corn, peas
- Most fruits (except small amounts of berries)
- Sugar, sweets, juices, milk in large amounts

That removes most of a typical South Indian diet.

# Pros of keto

- **Fast early weight loss** (2–4 kg in the first 1–2 weeks, mostly water)
- **Less hunger** for some people
- **Simple rule:** avoid carbs
- Can improve blood sugar in some people with type 2 diabetes, **under medical supervision**

# Cons of keto

- **Hard to follow** with Indian family meals and functions
- **"Keto flu"** in the first week: headache, tiredness, irritability
- **Low fibre:** constipation is common
- **LDL cholesterol rises** in some people, especially with lots of butter, ghee and cream
- **Hard to train hard:** gym performance often drops at first
- **Regain:** weight often returns when carbs come back
- **Expensive:** more meat, paneer, nuts and specialty products

# Does keto work better than other diets?

Studies comparing keto with other diets at **similar calories** usually find **similar fat loss** after 6–12 months. The early difference is mostly water.

So keto works if you enjoy it and can stick to it. It is not magic. Compare it with others in [types of diet](/blog/types-of-diet).

# Who should avoid keto (or only do it with a doctor)

- **Diabetes on insulin, sulfonylureas or SGLT2 inhibitor tablets** (risk of low sugar or ketoacidosis)
- **Kidney disease** or a history of kidney stones
- **Liver or pancreas disease**
- **Pregnancy or breastfeeding**
- **High cholesterol or heart disease**
- **History of eating disorders**

# A gentler option: low-carb, high-protein Indian diet

Many people get most of the benefits with a **moderate low-carb approach** that keeps Indian food:

- **1 cup rice or 2 chapatis per meal**, not 3–4
- **Protein at every meal:** eggs, chicken, fish, paneer, tofu, dal
- **Half the plate vegetables**
- **No sugar, juices or sweets** most days

This is easier to follow, keeps fibre, and still creates a calorie deficit. See [how to lose weight in 30 days](/blog/how-to-lose-weight-in-30-days).

# Sample Indian keto day (for reference)

- **Breakfast:** 3-egg omelette with spinach and cheese
- **Lunch:** grilled chicken or paneer, cabbage poriyal, cucumber salad, a spoon of coconut chutney
- **Snack:** a handful of almonds
- **Dinner:** fish curry (coconut base), cauliflower stir-fry

# Frequently asked questions

**How much weight can I lose on keto in a month?**
Often 3–6 kg on the scale, but 1–3 kg of that is water that returns when you eat carbs again.

**Is keto safe long-term?**
Long-term safety is less studied. Get blood tests (cholesterol, liver, kidney) if you stay on it for months.

**Can vegetarians do keto in India?**
Yes, with paneer, cheese, tofu, nuts and low-carb vegetables, but it is harder and more restrictive.
