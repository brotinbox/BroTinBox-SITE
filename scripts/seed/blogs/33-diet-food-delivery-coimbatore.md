# What "diet food" should actually mean

A lot of food sold as "diet" is just small portions or boiled vegetables. Real diet food for weight loss or muscle gain has four things:

1. **Enough protein.** Protein keeps you full and protects your muscle while you lose fat. A good diet meal has at least 30–40 g.
2. **Controlled carbs, not zero carbs.** Rice, chapati or oats in a measured amount. Cutting carbs completely usually ends in a binge.
3. **Less oil.** Oil is the hidden source of calories in Indian food. One tablespoon is about 120 kcal.
4. **Known numbers.** If you do not know the calories and protein, you are guessing.

If you want to understand the numbers behind this, our [calorie deficit guide](/blog/calorie-deficit-how-to-calculate) explains how to work out how much to eat.
