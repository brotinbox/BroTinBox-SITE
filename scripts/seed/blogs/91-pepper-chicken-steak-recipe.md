> **Quick answer:** For a **pepper chicken steak** at home, flatten **chicken breast** to an even 1.5 cm, rub with **freshly crushed black pepper, garlic, salt, lemon juice and 1 tsp oil**, rest for 20–30 minutes, then sear in a **very hot pan for 4–5 minutes per side** until cooked through. Rest it for 5 minutes before slicing. A **150 g cooked steak** gives about **45–46 g protein and 260–290 kcal**.

A chicken steak is simply a whole, flattened piece of chicken cooked like a steak: seared hard on the outside, juicy inside. This is a home-style version of the **Pepper Chicken Steak** on our BroTin Box menu, with a bold Kongu-style pepper kick.

# Ingredients (serves 2)

**Chicken**

- 2 boneless chicken breasts (about 400 g raw in total)

**Pepper rub**

- 1½ tsp black peppercorns, freshly crushed (coarse, not powder)
- 4 garlic cloves, finely grated
- ½ tsp ginger paste
- ½ tsp salt (adjust)
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp oil

**To finish (optional)**

- 1 tsp butter or ghee
- A few curry leaves
- A pinch more crushed pepper

# Method

## 1. Flatten the chicken

Place each breast between two sheets of butter paper or a plastic cover. Pound gently with a rolling pin or the base of a heavy vessel until it is an even **1.5 cm thick**.

This is the most important step. Even thickness means the steak cooks evenly and stays juicy.

## 2. Make the rub

Crush peppercorns in a mortar and pestle (or pulse briefly in a mixer). Mix with garlic, ginger, salt, turmeric, cumin, lemon juice and oil.

## 3. Season and rest

Rub the mixture all over the chicken. Rest **20–30 minutes** at room temperature, or up to 4 hours in the fridge.

## 4. Sear

- Heat a heavy iron pan or grill pan until **very hot** (a drop of water should sizzle instantly).
- Add a few drops of oil.
- Place the steaks and **do not move them for 4–5 minutes**, until a dark crust forms.
- Flip and cook **4–5 minutes more**.

## 5. Finish and rest

- In the last minute, add butter or ghee and curry leaves, and spoon the foaming fat over the chicken (optional).
- Check the thickest part: it should be **white all the way through** with clear juices, or **74°C** on a thermometer.
- **Rest for 5 minutes**, then slice against the grain.

# Nutrition per serving

For one **150 g cooked steak**:

- **Calories:** about 260–290 kcal (add about 35 kcal if finished with 1 tsp butter)
- **Protein:** about 45–46 g
- **Fat:** about 6–9 g
- **Carbs:** about 2 g

# Tips for a juicy steak

- **Pat dry** before adding the rub. A wet surface steams instead of searing.
- **Hot pan, little oil.** The crust is where the flavour is.
- **Do not press it down** with the spatula; you squeeze out the juices.
- **Rest before slicing.** Cutting immediately lets the juices run out.
- **Thigh option:** boneless thighs make an even juicier steak; cook 5–6 minutes per side.

# Other ways to cook it

- **Oven:** sear 2 minutes per side, then 200°C for 8–10 minutes.
- **Air fryer:** 200°C for 12–14 minutes, flipping halfway. See [grilled vs air fried](/blog/grilled-vs-air-fried).

# Frequently asked questions

**Why is my chicken steak tough?**
It is usually overcooked or uneven in thickness. Flatten it evenly and take it off the heat as soon as it is done.

**Can I use pepper powder?**
Yes, but freshly crushed pepper gives a much stronger, more aromatic flavour and a better crust.

**Is pepper chicken good for weight loss?**
Yes. It is high in protein, low in fat and full of flavour without a heavy gravy.
