> **Quick answer:** A good weight loss diet for Indian women is usually about **1,300–1,700 kcal a day** (depending on height, weight and activity), with **1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg** of body weight, **half the plate vegetables**, measured rice or chapati, and enough **iron and calcium**. Add **strength training 2–3 times a week** and **7,000–10,000 steps a day**. Expect about **0.5–0.75 kg a week**, with normal water swings around your period.

Most weight loss advice is written for men. Women's needs are a little different: lower calorie needs, monthly water changes, higher iron needs, and often conditions like PCOS or thyroid. Here is a plan that fits.

# How many calories do women need to lose weight?

A rough guide for a moderately active Indian woman:

- **Maintenance:** about body weight (kg) × 26–30
- **Weight loss:** subtract **300–500 kcal**

**Example:** a 70 kg woman with a desk job. Maintenance about 70 × 27 = 1,890 kcal. Target: **about 1,400–1,600 kcal**.

Do not go below **about 1,200 kcal** without medical guidance. See [how to calculate a calorie deficit](/blog/calorie-deficit-how-to-calculate).

# Protein is the key

Many Indian women eat only 40–50 g of protein a day. For weight loss, aim for **1.2–1.6 g per kg**, so about **80–100 g** for a 65–70 kg woman.

Easy sources: eggs, curd, paneer, dal, chana, sprouts, chicken, fish, tofu.

Read our guide on [protein for Indian women](/blog/protein-for-indian-women).

# Nutrients women should watch

- **Iron:** low iron is very common in Indian women and causes tiredness. Eat greens, dal, chana, eggs, meat and fish, with vitamin C (lemon, amla).
- **Calcium:** curd, milk, ragi, paneer, sesame.
- **Vitamin D:** often low in India; ask your doctor to test it.
- **Vitamin B12:** especially for vegetarians.

# A sample 1,500 kcal day

- **Breakfast:** 2 idlis with sambar and 2 boiled eggs (or moong dal chilla with curd)
- **Mid-morning:** a fruit
- **Lunch:** 1 cup rice, dal, 100 g paneer or chicken, poriyal, salad, curd
- **Evening:** tea without sugar and a cup of sprouts or sundal
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal or egg curry, vegetables

About **1,500 kcal and 80–90 g protein**.

# Exercise for women

- **Strength training 2–3 times a week:** it will **not** make you bulky. It keeps muscle, shapes your body, and protects bones.
- **Walking:** 7,000–10,000 steps a day.
- **Optional cardio:** cycling, dance, swimming, or the [12-3-30 treadmill workout](/blog/12-3-30-treadmill-workout).

# Periods and the scale

In the week before and during your period, many women **retain 1–2 kg of water**. Cravings and hunger can also rise.

- **Do not panic** if the scale jumps
- Compare your weight to the **same week last month**
- Keep protein high and allow a little extra for cravings rather than bingeing

# PCOS and thyroid

**PCOS**

- Weight loss can be slower, but it works
- A high-protein, higher-fibre diet with measured carbs often helps
- Strength training improves insulin sensitivity
- Work with your doctor, especially if you take metformin or other medicines

**Hypothyroidism**

- Take your thyroid tablet as prescribed, on an empty stomach
- Once your levels are normal, weight loss works the same way: a calorie deficit

# After pregnancy

Wait until your doctor clears you, usually 6–8 weeks after delivery. If breastfeeding, avoid large deficits; aim for slow loss of about 0.5 kg a week, and keep protein and calcium high.

# Common mistakes

- **Eating too little:** 800–1,000 kcal plans cause hair fall, fatigue and regain
- **Skipping protein**
- **Only cardio, no strength training**
- **Judging progress during your period week**
- **Following men's plans** with calorie targets that are too high or too low

# Frequently asked questions

**How much weight can a woman lose in a month?**
Usually 2–3 kg of fat, plus some early water loss. See [how to lose weight in 30 days](/blog/how-to-lose-weight-in-30-days).

**Will lifting weights make me bulky?**
No. Women have much less testosterone; lifting makes you toned and stronger.

**Is rice okay for women trying to lose weight?**
Yes, in a measured portion of about 1 cup cooked per meal.
