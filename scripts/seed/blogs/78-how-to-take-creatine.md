> **Quick answer:** Take **3–5 g of creatine monohydrate every day**, including rest days, mixed in water, milk or a shake. **Timing does not matter much**; taking it around your workout or with a meal is fine. **Loading** (about **20 g a day split into 4 doses for 5–7 days**) fills your muscles faster but is optional. Without loading, 3–5 g a day reaches the same level in about **3–4 weeks**. You do **not** need to cycle off.

Creatine is simple to take, but the internet makes it confusing with loading phases, timing rules and "cycles". Here is a clear guide.

New to creatine? Read [is creatine good for health](/blog/is-creatine-good-for-health) first.

# Which creatine should you buy?

**Creatine monohydrate.** It is the form used in almost all the research.

- **Micronised monohydrate:** the same thing ground finer, so it mixes better. Fine to use.
- **Creatine HCl, ethyl ester, buffered, "liquid creatine":** more expensive and not proven to work better.

Buy unflavoured monohydrate from a trusted seller with an **FSSAI licence** and batch details on the pack.

# How much to take

**Daily dose:** 3–5 g a day.

- Most people: **3–5 g** (about one level teaspoon)
- Very large, muscular people: up to 5 g is enough

More is not better. Your muscles can only store so much; the extra is passed out in urine.

# Should you do a loading phase?

**Loading** means taking more for the first week to fill your muscles fast.

- **How:** about **0.3 g per kg of body weight a day** (around 20 g for a 65–70 kg person), split into **4 doses of 5 g**, for **5–7 days**
- **Then:** drop to 3–5 g a day

**Without loading:** take 3–5 g a day from day 1. Muscles are fully topped up in about 3–4 weeks.

**Which is better?** Both end in the same place. Loading is faster but can cause stomach upset and more early water weight. Most people can simply skip it.

# When to take creatine

Research shows **only small differences** between taking it before or after training. What matters is taking it **every day**.

Good options:

- **Right after your workout** with your post-workout meal or shake
- **With breakfast** if that is easier to remember
- **On rest days:** any time, with a meal

Taking it **with a meal that has carbs and protein** may slightly improve how much your muscles absorb.

# How to mix it

- Stir into **water, milk, buttermilk, juice or a protein shake**
- It does not fully dissolve in cold water; that is normal. Stir and drink
- Warm water helps it dissolve better

**Do not "dry scoop"** (swallowing the powder without water). It is a social media trend that can make you choke or inhale powder.

# Do you need to cycle creatine?

No. There is no evidence that taking breaks is needed. Long-term daily use has been studied and found safe in healthy people.

If you stop, your muscle creatine slowly returns to normal over about 4–6 weeks.

# Water and creatine

- Drink **normally**, enough that your urine is pale yellow
- In Coimbatore's hot months, and on heavy training days, drink a little more
- You do not need several extra litres

# What to expect

- **Week 1–2:** about 1–2 kg up on the scale, mainly water inside the muscles (faster if you load)
- **Week 2–4:** slightly more reps and better recovery
- **Months:** more strength and muscle than training alone

Some people notice little change. These "non-responders" usually already had high creatine levels, often because they eat a lot of meat and fish. See [foods that contain creatine](/blog/foods-that-contain-creatine).

# Common mistakes

- **Taking it only on training days.** Take it daily.
- **Mega-dosing** 10–20 g every day for months. Not needed.
- **Buying expensive forms** that are not better than monohydrate.
- **Expecting it to work without training or protein.**
- **Stopping because of water weight.** It is not fat.

# Safety notes

Creatine is safe for healthy adults at 3–5 g a day. Talk to a doctor first if you have kidney disease, diabetes with kidney involvement, or take regular medicines. Read [does creatine cause kidney problems](/blog/does-creatine-cause-kidney-problems).

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I take creatine with whey protein?**
Yes. Mixing them in one shake is fine.

**Can I take creatine with coffee?**
Yes. Earlier concerns about caffeine cancelling creatine are not well supported.

**Is creatine okay for women?**
Yes. Same dose, same benefits.
