> **Quick answer:** A commonly used guide puts **healthy body fat for adult men at about 14–24%** and for **adult women at about 21–31%**. **Athletic** levels are about **6–13% (men)** and **14–20% (women)**. Above about **25% (men)** or **32% (women)** is usually considered obese. Women naturally carry more fat than men. Home scales and gym machines give **rough estimates**; track the **trend over months**, along with your **waist and photos**, rather than a single number.

BMI does not tell you how much of your weight is fat. Body fat percentage does. Here is what is normal, how to measure it, and what to aim for.

# What is body fat percentage?

It is the share of your body weight that is fat.

**Example:** 80 kg with 20% body fat = 16 kg of fat and 64 kg of everything else (muscle, bone, water, organs).

Some fat is **essential**: for hormones, organs and nerves. Women need more essential fat than men.

# Body fat ranges

A widely used guide (from the American Council on Exercise) is:

**Men**

- Essential fat: 2–5%
- Athletes: 6–13%
- Fitness: 14–17%
- Average: 18–24%
- Obese: 25% and above

**Women**

- Essential fat: 10–13%
- Athletes: 14–20%
- Fitness: 21–24%
- Average: 25–31%
- Obese: 32% and above

**For Indians:** research suggests South Asians tend to have **higher body fat at the same BMI** and more fat around the belly, so waist size matters a lot. See [what is BMI](/blog/what-is-bmi).

# What does "visible abs" need?

Roughly:

- **Men:** around 10–12% or lower
- **Women:** around 16–19% or lower

These levels are lean and not necessary for health. Read [how to get abs](/blog/how-to-get-abs).

# How to measure body fat

**DEXA scan**

- Accuracy: high
- Cost: expensive, available in some hospitals and labs

**Skinfold calipers**

- Accuracy: good if done by a trained person
- Cost: cheap

**Smart scales and gym machines (bioelectrical impedance)**

- Accuracy: rough; affected by water, food and time of day
- Cost: cheap or free at gyms

**Waist measurement and the US Navy formula**

- Accuracy: rough estimate
- Cost: free with a measuring tape

**Photos**

- Accuracy: not a number, but very useful for tracking change

**Tip:** whatever method you use, measure **the same way, at the same time of day, every 2–4 weeks**, and watch the trend.

# Waist: the simplest health check

- **Indian men:** 90 cm or more suggests higher risk
- **Indian women:** 80 cm or more suggests higher risk
- **Waist less than half your height** is a good target

# How to lower body fat

- **Calorie deficit** of about 300–500 kcal
- **High protein:** 1.2–2 g per kg
- **Strength training** to keep muscle
- **Daily steps:** 8,000–10,000
- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours

See [fat loss vs weight loss](/blog/fat-loss-vs-weight-loss) and [how to lose weight in 30 days](/blog/how-to-lose-weight-in-30-days).

# How fast can body fat drop?

Roughly **0.5–1% body fat per month** for many people with a consistent plan, faster at higher levels.

# Can body fat be too low?

Yes. Very low body fat can cause:

- Hormone problems and missed periods in women
- Low energy, poor mood and sleep
- Weakened immunity

Stage-ready bodybuilders hold very low levels only briefly.

# Frequently asked questions

**What is a healthy body fat for a 30-year-old Indian man?**
Around 14–22% is a reasonable healthy range, with a waist under 90 cm.

**Are smart scale readings accurate?**
They can be off by several percent. Use them only to watch the trend.

**Can I lose fat and gain muscle at the same time?**
Yes, especially as a beginner, with high protein and strength training.
