Gaining muscle is mostly about eating enough protein and calories, every single day. That is hard with a busy job or college schedule. Here is how to plan it in Coimbatore, whether you order in or cook.

# What a muscle gain meal plan needs

- **Calories:** maintenance + 300–500 kcal. See [calories to build muscle](/blog/calories-to-build-muscle).
- **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g per kg. For 70 kg, that is about **110–150 g a day**.
- **Carbs:** enough to fuel training: rice, chapati, oats, millets, fruit
- **Fats:** from nuts, eggs, fish, a little ghee
- **Consistency:** 7 days a week, not just training days

- **Kollu and chana sundal:** traditional Kongu protein snacks
- **Paneer and curd:** easy veg protein

# Mistakes to avoid

- **Only one big meal a day** with protein
- **Dirty bulking** on parotta, biryani and bakery items. Read [dirty bulk vs clean bulk](/blog/dirty-bulk-vs-clean-bulk).
- **Eating big only on training days**
- **Skipping breakfast** and then struggling to catch up

# How much will you gain?

At a 300–500 kcal surplus, about **0.25–0.5 kg a week**, of which a good share is muscle if you train well. See [how to bulk in 60 days](/blog/how-to-bulk-in-60-days).
