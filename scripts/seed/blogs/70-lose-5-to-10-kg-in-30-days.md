> **Quick answer:** **Losing 5–10 kg of fat in 30 days is not realistic or safe for most people.** One kilo of body fat holds about **7,700 kcal**, so losing 10 kg of fat in a month would need a deficit of over 2,500 kcal every day. What you **can** do in 30 days: lose about **2–4 kg of fat** plus **1–2 kg of water**, so **3–5 kg on the scale** for most people, and sometimes more if you start very heavy. This guide shows how to get the most from 30 days without crashing.

Reels and ads often promise "lose 10 kg in 30 days". Here is the honest maths, what is actually possible, and a plan to get the best safe result.

# The maths of fat loss

- **1 kg of body fat ≈ 7,700 kcal**
- To lose **5 kg of fat** in 30 days: about **1,280 kcal deficit a day**
- To lose **10 kg of fat** in 30 days: about **2,570 kcal deficit a day**

Most adults only burn 1,800–2,800 kcal a day in total. A 2,500 kcal daily deficit would mean eating almost nothing, which is neither safe nor sustainable.

# So how do people "lose 10 kg in a month"?

When you see big numbers, usually:

- **A lot of it is water.** Cutting carbs and salt drops 2–4 kg of water fast.
- **Some is food in the gut.** Eating less means less weight in your digestive system.
- **Some is muscle.** Starvation diets burn muscle.
- **They started very heavy.** A 130 kg person loses faster than a 70 kg person.
- **It comes back.** Most of this returns within weeks of eating normally.

# What is a safe rate?

A common guideline is **0.5–1 kg a week**, or about **0.5–1% of your body weight a week**.

- 70 kg person: about 0.35–0.7 kg a week
- 100 kg person: about 0.5–1 kg a week

Over 30 days, that is roughly **2–4 kg of fat**, plus the early water drop.

# Who might lose 5 kg or more in 30 days

- People starting at **100 kg or more**
- People cutting out large amounts of sugar, fried food and alcohol
- People on a **doctor-supervised** programme

For them, 5–6 kg on the scale in a month can happen. 10 kg is still unusual and should be monitored by a doctor.

# The best 30-day plan for maximum safe weight loss

## 1. Eat 500–750 kcal below maintenance

- Estimate maintenance: body weight (kg) × 25–28 for a sitting job
- Subtract 500–750 kcal
- Do not go below about 1,200 kcal (women) or 1,500 kcal (men) without medical advice

See [how to calculate a calorie deficit](/blog/calorie-deficit-how-to-calculate).

## 2. Eat high protein

**1.2–1.6 g per kg** of body weight. This keeps you full and protects muscle, so the weight you lose is fat.

## 3. Cut liquid and hidden calories

- No sugary drinks, juices or sugar in tea
- Measure oil: 1 tbsp = 120 kcal
- Limit fried snacks and sweets

## 4. Walk 10,000 steps a day

Walking is the easiest way to burn an extra 300–400 kcal a day. See [walking for weight loss](/blog/walking-for-weight-loss-steps).

## 5. Lift weights 3 times a week

Strength training tells your body to keep muscle while you lose fat.

## 6. Sleep 7–8 hours

Poor sleep increases hunger and cravings.

# A sample 1,500 kcal day

- **Breakfast:** 2 boiled eggs, 1 cup oats upma or 2 idlis with sambar
- **Lunch:** 150 g grilled chicken or paneer, 1 cup rice, poriyal, salad
- **Evening:** buttermilk and a cup of sprouts or sundal
- **Dinner:** 2 chapatis, dal, vegetables

About **1,500 kcal and 90–100 g protein**.

# Warning signs you are going too fast

- Dizziness, weakness, or feeling cold all the time
- Hair fall a few months later
- Missing periods
- Constant hunger and binge eating
- Strength dropping fast in the gym

If you notice these, eat more and slow down. Very fast weight loss also increases the risk of **gallstones**.

# What to aim for instead

- **30 days:** 3–5 kg on the scale, a smaller waist, better habits
- **60 days:** 5–8 kg for most people
- **90 days:** 7–12 kg for many people with more to lose

Slow, steady loss is the loss that stays off. If you stall along the way, read [why the scale stopped moving](/blog/weight-loss-plateau-why-scale-stopped).

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I lose 5 kg in 30 days?**
Some people can, especially if they start heavy. For most, 3–5 kg on the scale is a great month.

**Is losing 10 kg in a month dangerous?**
For most people, yes. It usually means extreme dieting, muscle loss and quick regain.

**Will I regain the water weight?**
Some of it, when you eat more carbs again. That is normal and not fat gain.
