> **Quick answer:** For **South Indian grilled mackerel (kaanaangeluthi)**, clean 2 medium fish, make deep slits, and coat with a paste of **Kashmiri chilli, turmeric, pepper, coriander powder, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice and salt**. Rest for **30 minutes**, then cook on a hot tawa with **1–2 tsp oil** for about **4–5 minutes per side**, adding curry leaves at the end. A **130 g cooked portion** has about **305 kcal and 31 g protein**, plus omega-3 fats.

Mackerel is one of the healthiest and most affordable fish in Tamil Nadu. This is a home-style version of the **Crispy Grilled Mackerel** on our BroTin Box menu: crisp outside, juicy inside, and made with very little oil.

# Ingredients (serves 2)

**Fish**

- 2 medium mackerel (kaanaangeluthi), about 500–600 g whole
- Ask the fish shop to clean and gut them, or do it at home

**Masala**

- 1½ tsp Kashmiri chilli powder (colour)
- ½ tsp regular chilli powder (heat, optional)
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp black pepper powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (or thick tamarind water)
- ½ tsp salt (adjust)
- 1 tbsp rice flour (for crispness, optional)
- 1–2 tbsp water, just enough to make a thick paste

**For cooking**

- 1–2 tsp oil (coconut oil gives a Kerala-style flavour)
- A sprig of curry leaves

# Method

## 1. Clean the fish

- Wash the fish well in water with a pinch of turmeric and salt, then rinse.
- Pat completely dry with a cloth. Dry fish crisps better.
- Make **2–3 deep diagonal slits** on each side, down to the bone.

## 2. Make the masala

Mix all the masala ingredients into a thick paste. It should not drip.

## 3. Coat and rest

Rub the masala all over the fish and **into the slits**. Rest for **30 minutes** (up to 2 hours in the fridge).

## 4. Cook on a tawa

- Heat a heavy iron tawa or non-stick pan on medium-high.
- Spread 1 tsp oil.
- Place the fish and cook **4–5 minutes** without moving it, until the bottom is crisp and brown.
- Flip carefully with a flat spatula, add a few drops of oil around the fish, and cook **4–5 minutes more**.
- Add curry leaves to the pan in the last minute.

The fish is done when the flesh near the bone turns opaque and flakes easily.

## 5. Oven or air fryer option

- **Oven:** 220°C for 15–18 minutes on a greased tray, then grill for 2–3 minutes.
- **Air fryer:** 200°C for 10–12 minutes, turning once.

# Nutrition per serving

For a **130 g cooked portion** (about one medium fish without head and bones):

- **Calories:** about 305 kcal
- **Protein:** about 31 g
- **Omega-3 fats (EPA and DHA):** a good amount; mackerel is an oily fish

# Tips for perfect grilled fish

- **Buy fresh:** bright eyes, red gills, firm flesh that springs back
- **Dry the fish well** before adding masala
- **Do not flip too early;** wait until the crust forms
- **Use a heavy pan;** thin pans cause sticking and uneven cooking
- **Remove bones carefully** when serving to children

# Variations

- **Sardines (mathi):** same masala, cook 3–4 minutes per side
- **Seer fish (vanjiram) slices:** same masala, 3–4 minutes per side
- **Green masala:** swap the chilli powder for a paste of mint, coriander, green chilli and garlic

# Frequently asked questions

**How often should I eat mackerel?**
About two servings of fish a week is a common healthy target.

**Is grilled mackerel good for weight loss?**
Yes, in a measured portion. It is high in protein and keeps you full. Watch the oil.

**Does mackerel have mercury?**
Small mackerel species like the Indian mackerel are generally lower in mercury than large predatory fish. Two servings a week is considered safe for most people.
