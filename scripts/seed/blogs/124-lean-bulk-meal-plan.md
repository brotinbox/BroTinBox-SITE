> **Quick answer:** A **lean bulk** means gaining muscle with minimal fat, using a **small surplus of about 200–300 kcal a day** and **1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg**. Aim to gain about **0.25–0.5% of your body weight a week** (roughly 0.75–1.5 kg a month for a 70 kg person). A good Indian lean bulk day for a 70 kg lifter is about **2,600–2,800 kcal with 130–150 g protein**, built on eggs, chicken or fish, paneer or soya, dal, rice, chapati, curd, fruit and nuts.

A dirty bulk adds muscle and a lot of fat. A lean bulk is slower, but you stay in shape and spend less time cutting afterwards. Here is a complete Indian lean bulk meal plan.

# Lean bulk vs regular bulk

**Lean bulk**

- Surplus: about 200–300 kcal
- Gain: about 0.25–0.5% of body weight a week
- Fat gain: low
- Best for: people who are already fairly lean, or intermediate lifters

**Regular or aggressive bulk**

- Surplus: 400–600+ kcal
- Gain: faster
- Fat gain: higher
- Best for: very skinny beginners. See [how to bulk in 60 days](/blog/how-to-bulk-in-60-days).

Read more in [dirty bulk vs clean bulk](/blog/dirty-bulk-vs-clean-bulk).

# Set your numbers

1. **Maintenance:** track for 1–2 weeks, or estimate with [how many calories should I eat](/blog/how-many-calories-should-i-eat)
2. **Lean bulk calories:** maintenance + 200–300 kcal
3. **Protein:** 1.6–2.2 g per kg
4. **Fats:** about 0.8–1 g per kg
5. **Carbs:** the rest, to fuel training

**Example (70 kg, maintenance 2,450 kcal):**

- Calories: about 2,700 kcal
- Protein: about 140 g
- Fat: about 65 g
- Carbs: about 360 g

# Lean bulk meal plan (non-veg, about 2,700 kcal)

**7:30 AM, breakfast**

- 3 whole eggs + 2 egg whites, omelette with onion and tomato
- 2 chapatis
- 1 banana
(about 550 kcal, 32 g protein)

**11 AM, snack**

- 1 cup curd with 2 dates and a handful of peanuts (25 g)
(about 300 kcal, 14 g protein)

**1:30 PM, lunch**

- 150 g grilled chicken
- 1.5 cups rice
- Dal, vegetables, salad
(about 750 kcal, 55 g protein)

**5 PM, pre-workout**

- 1 cup oats with milk or 2 bananas
(about 300 kcal, 10 g protein)

**8:30 PM, dinner**

- 150 g fish (mackerel or seer) or paneer
- 2 chapatis, dal or sundal, vegetables
(about 650 kcal, 40 g protein)

**10 PM (optional)**

- A glass of milk
(about 150 kcal, 8 g protein)

**Total: about 2,700 kcal and 150+ g protein**

About **2,600–2,700 kcal and 120–135 g protein**. Add a scoop of whey if needed.

# Training for a lean bulk

- **4–5 sessions a week**, each muscle trained twice
- **Progressive overload:** add weight or reps every week
- **Compound lifts first,** then accessories

# Track and adjust every 2 weeks

- **Gaining faster than 0.5% a week** and waist growing? Cut 100–150 kcal
- **Not gaining for 2 weeks?** Add 100–150 kcal
- **Strength going up and waist stable?** Perfect

# Tips

- **Eat the same breakfast and lunch** most days; vary dinner
- **Do not skip meals** on rest days
- **Sleep 7–9 hours.** See [sleep and muscle growth](/blog/sleep-and-muscle-growth).
- **Creatine can help:** see [how to take creatine](/blog/how-to-take-creatine)

# Frequently asked questions

**How long should a lean bulk last?**
Usually 3–6 months or longer, until you want to cut or reach your goal.

**Can beginners lean bulk?**
Yes, though skinny beginners often do better with a slightly bigger surplus.

**Is rice okay on a lean bulk?**
Yes. Rice is an easy, clean carb for training.
