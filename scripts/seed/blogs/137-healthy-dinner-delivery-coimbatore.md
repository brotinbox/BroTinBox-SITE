# What makes a healthy dinner?

- **Protein:** 25–40 g, so you are not raiding the kitchen at 11 PM
- **Vegetables or salad**
- **Measured carbs:** 1–2 chapatis or a small portion of rice or oats
- **Light on oil and not too heavy,** so you sleep well
- **Eaten 2–3 hours before bed** if possible

# If you cook dinner at home

A simple high-protein dinner:

- **Protein:** 150 g chicken, fish, paneer or tofu, or 2–3 eggs
- **Vegetables:** poriyal, salad, or a soup
- **Carb:** 2 chapatis or ½–1 cup rice
- **Curd** on the side if it suits you
