> **Quick answer:** For **tandoori tofu tikka**, **press 200 g firm tofu** for 15–20 minutes, cut into cubes, and marinate in **hung curd (or thick coconut yoghurt for vegan), roasted besan, Kashmiri chilli, ginger-garlic, garam masala, kasuri methi, lemon and 1 tsp oil** for 30 minutes. Cook on a hot tawa for **2–3 minutes per side**, or bake at **220°C for 15–18 minutes**. A **125 g tofu serving** gives about **15–21 g protein for 190–230 kcal**, fewer calories than paneer tikka.

Tofu tikka is the lighter cousin of paneer tikka. Pressed well and marinated right, tofu takes on the same smoky tandoori flavour with far fewer calories. This is a home-style version of the **Tandoori Tofu Tikka** on our BroTin Box veg menu.

# Ingredients (serves 2)

**Tofu and vegetables**

- 250 g firm or extra-firm tofu
- 1 onion, cut into petals
- 1 capsicum, cut into squares

**Marinade**

- ½ cup thick hung curd (for vegan: thick unsweetened coconut or soy yoghurt)
- 1 tbsp besan, dry roasted for 1 minute
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- ½ tsp regular chilli powder (optional)
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp kasuri methi, crushed
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp mustard oil (or any oil)
- Salt to taste

# Method

## 1. Press the tofu

This is the step most people skip, and it makes all the difference.

- Wrap the tofu block in a clean cloth or kitchen towel.
- Place it on a plate and put something heavy on top (a pan with a few cans or a mortar).
- Press for **15–20 minutes**. Water will come out.

Pressed tofu is firmer, browns better and absorbs more marinade.

## 2. Cut and marinate

- Cut into 2.5–3 cm cubes.
- Mix all marinade ingredients into a thick paste.
- Gently coat the tofu, onion and capsicum.
- Rest **30 minutes** (up to 4 hours in the fridge).

## 3. Thread

Thread tofu, onion and capsicum onto skewers. Soak wooden skewers in water for 20 minutes first.

## 4. Cook

**Tawa or grill pan:** brush with a few drops of oil, cook **2–3 minutes per side** on medium-high until charred spots appear. Turn gently with tongs.

**Oven:** 220°C for **15–18 minutes** on a rack, turning once, then grill for 2 minutes.

**Air fryer:** 200°C for **10–12 minutes**, turning halfway.

## 5. Finish

Sprinkle chaat masala and lemon. Serve with mint chutney and onion rings.

# Nutrition per serving

For about **125 g firm tofu** with marinade and vegetables:

- **Calories:** about 190–230 kcal
- **Protein:** about 15–21 g (depends on the tofu brand)
- **Fat:** about 10–12 g
- **Carbs:** about 10–12 g

Compared with the same amount of paneer tikka, that is **roughly half the calories**. See [paneer vs tofu](/blog/paneer-vs-tofu).

# Tips

- **Use firm or extra-firm tofu.** Soft or silken tofu will fall apart.
- **Check the label** for protein per 100 g; it varies a lot between brands.
- **Do not over-handle.** Turn gently so the cubes stay whole.
- **Smoky flavour:** use the charcoal and ghee "dhungar" method from our [paneer tikka recipe](/blog/tandoori-paneer-tikka-recipe), in a ventilated kitchen.

# Is tofu healthy?

Yes. Tofu is a complete plant protein, low in saturated fat, and often a good source of calcium if made with calcium sulphate. Normal food amounts of soy are safe for men and women. People with a soy allergy should avoid it.

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I make tofu tikka without curd?**
Yes. Use thick coconut or soy yoghurt, or a paste of besan, lemon and spices.

**Why does my tofu taste bland?**
It was not pressed enough, or not marinated long enough. Press well and marinate at least 30 minutes.

**Is tofu tikka good for weight loss?**
Yes. It gives good protein for few calories.
