> **Quick answer:** For a **smashed chicken bowl**, gently **boil 500 g boneless chicken** with salt, pepper, bay leaf and garlic for **15–18 minutes**, let it cool slightly, then **shred and smash it** with two forks. Toss with **rock salt, freshly crushed black pepper, lemon, fresh herbs (coriander, mint) and a little of the cooking stock**. No frying needed. A **150 g serving** has about **45–47 g protein and 250–310 kcal**, making it one of the leanest high-protein meals you can make.

This is the simplest high-protein dish on our menu, and one of the most popular. Boiled chicken sounds boring, but shredded and seasoned right it is soft, peppery and full of flavour. Here is a home-style version of our **Smashed Chicken Bowl**.

# Ingredients (serves 3)

**For boiling**

- 500 g boneless chicken breast (or half breast, half thigh)
- 4 cups water (enough to cover)
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp whole black peppercorns
- 1 bay leaf
- 4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- 1 small onion, halved
- 1 inch ginger, sliced

**For tossing**

- 1 tsp freshly crushed black pepper
- Rock salt to taste
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp chopped coriander
- 1 tbsp chopped mint
- 1 green chilli, finely chopped (optional)
- 3–4 tbsp of the chicken cooking stock
- Optional: 1 tsp butter or olive oil for richness

# Method

## 1. Poach the chicken gently

- Put the chicken, water and all boiling ingredients in a pot.
- Bring to a boil, then **lower the heat to a gentle simmer**.
- Cover and cook **15–18 minutes** until the chicken is white all the way through.

**Tip:** a hard boil makes chicken tough. A gentle simmer keeps it soft.

## 2. Rest

Turn off the heat and let the chicken sit in the hot stock for **5 minutes**. Then remove it to a plate. **Save the stock.**

## 3. Shred and smash

While still warm, shred the chicken with **two forks**, then press and smash it lightly so the fibres break up and can soak in the seasoning. You can also pulse it 2–3 times in a mixer for a finer texture.

## 4. Season

Add crushed pepper, rock salt, lemon juice, herbs, green chilli and 3–4 tbsp of the warm stock. Toss well. Taste and adjust salt and pepper.

## 5. Serve

Serve warm or at room temperature.

# Nutrition per serving

For **150 g of shredded chicken breast**:

- **Calories:** about 250–270 kcal (our kitchen's version, with its seasoning, is about 310 kcal)
- **Protein:** about 45–47 g
- **Fat:** about 5–13 g, depending on the cut and whether you add butter
- **Carbs:** about 1–2 g

# Use the stock

The leftover stock is a light, tasty chicken broth. Use it:

- As a **clear soup** with pepper and coriander
- To cook rice or oats for more flavour
- As a base for rasam

# Why smashed chicken works for diets

- **No frying:** almost no added fat
- **Very high protein per calorie**
- **Soft texture:** easy to eat, great for older adults or after illness
- **Meal prep friendly:** keeps 2–3 days in the fridge

Use it in lunch boxes, wraps, salads and sandwiches through the week.

# Flavour variations

- **Kongu pepper:** add ½ tsp extra crushed pepper and fried curry leaves
- **Mint-lime:** double the mint and add lime zest
- **Chilli-garlic:** toss with 1 tsp chilli flakes and garlic sautéed in ½ tsp oil
- **Chettinad:** a pinch of Chettinad masala and fennel powder

# Frequently asked questions

**Is boiled chicken good for weight loss?**
Yes. It is one of the leanest, highest-protein foods you can eat.

**Why is my boiled chicken rubbery?**
It was boiled too hard or too long. Use a gentle simmer and stop as soon as it is cooked.

**Can I use chicken thigh?**
Yes. It will be juicier, with a little more fat and slightly less protein.
