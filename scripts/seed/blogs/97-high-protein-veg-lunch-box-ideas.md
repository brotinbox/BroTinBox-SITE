> **Quick answer:** Easy high-protein veg lunch boxes include **paneer tikka with chapati and chana (30–35 g protein)**, **soya chunk pulao with raita (30–35 g)**, **rajma rice with curd (20–25 g)**, **tofu bhurji wraps (25 g)**, **moong dal chilla rolls (20–25 g)** and **curd rice with sprouts and roasted chana (18–22 g)**. Build every box with **one main protein (paneer, tofu, soya)** plus **one legume or dairy side** to reach **25–35 g of protein**.

Most veg lunch boxes in India are mostly carbs: rice with a little sambar, or chapati with potato. Here are 12 vegetarian lunch box ideas that pack real protein, travel well and taste good at 1 PM.

# The formula

Every box below follows one rule:

1. **Main protein:** paneer, tofu, soya chunks, or a big portion of dal or rajma
2. **Protein side:** curd, chana, sprouts, or roasted peanuts
3. **Vegetables or salad**
4. **Measured carb:** chapati, rice, millet or oats

# 12 veg lunch box ideas

## 1. Paneer tikka, chapati and chana

100 g paneer tikka, 2 chapatis, a small box of black chana.
**About 30–35 g protein.** Recipe: [tandoori paneer tikka](/blog/tandoori-paneer-tikka-recipe).

## 2. Soya chunk pulao with raita

50 g dry soya chunks cooked into a vegetable pulao, cucumber raita.
**About 30–35 g protein.** See [soya chunks protein](/blog/soya-chunks-protein).

## 3. Rajma rice with curd

1.5 cups rajma, 1 cup rice, a cup of curd.
**About 20–25 g protein.**

## 4. Tofu bhurji wraps

150 g firm tofu scrambled with onion, tomato and spices, rolled in 2 chapatis.
**About 25 g protein.**

## 5. Moong dal chilla rolls

3 moong dal chillas stuffed with grated paneer, mint chutney.
**About 20–25 g protein.**

## 6. Chana masala with jeera rice and salad

1.5 cups chana masala, ¾ cup rice, onion-cucumber salad.
**About 18–22 g protein.**

## 7. Paneer bhurji with millet roti

100 g paneer bhurji, 2 ragi or jowar rotis, a small salad.
**About 22–25 g protein.**

## 8. Curd rice with sprouts and roasted chana

1 cup curd rice, a box of sprouts salad, a handful of roasted chana.
**About 18–22 g protein.**

## 9. Dal khichdi with curd and sundal

Moong dal and rice khichdi (more dal than rice), curd, kollu or chana sundal.
**About 20–25 g protein.**

## 10. Soya keema with chapati

50 g soya granules cooked like keema with peas, 2 chapatis.
**About 28–30 g protein.**

## 11. Paneer and vegetable fried rice (light oil)

75 g paneer cubes, mixed vegetables, 1 cup rice, cooked with 1 tsp oil.
**About 18–20 g protein.**

## 12. Sambar rice + paneer side

1 cup sambar rice, 75 g grilled paneer cubes, buttermilk.
**About 20–22 g protein.**

# Tips for packing

- **Keep curd and raita in a separate small box** so they do not make the rice soggy
- **Pack salad separately** and add lemon just before eating
- **Grill paneer or tofu** rather than frying; it stays firm
- **Use an insulated lunch bag** in Coimbatore's heat
- **Cook soya and dal in bulk** on Sunday for 2–3 days

# Prep once, pack all week

- **Sunday:** boil 500 g chana or rajma, soak and boil soya chunks, make a big batch of chapati dough
- **Each night:** grill paneer or make bhurji (10 minutes), pack with that day's carb and side

# Frequently asked questions

**Is paneer safe to carry in a lunch box?**
Yes, for a few hours. Keep it in an insulated bag on hot days, and eat it by lunch.

**Can vegetarians get 30 g of protein in one meal?**
Yes, with paneer, tofu or soya plus a legume or curd side.

**Are protein lunch boxes good for weight loss?**
Yes. Protein keeps you full and reduces afternoon snacking.
