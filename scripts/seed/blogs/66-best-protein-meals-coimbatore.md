Coimbatore has some of the best food in Tamil Nadu. But "best" for taste and "best" for protein are not always the same plate. Here are the meals that give you the most protein for the calories, whether you eat out, cook or order in.

# What makes a protein meal "the best"?

We rank meals on three things:

- **Protein:** at least 30 g per meal
- **Calories:** under about 700 kcal, so it fits most goals
- **Cooking method:** grilled, roasted, steamed or a light curry beats deep frying

# The best non-veg protein meals

## 1. Tandoori or grilled chicken with chapati and salad

- About **40–50 g protein**, 450–650 kcal (half tandoori chicken, 2 chapatis)
- Ask for less butter on the chapati
- One of the highest protein-per-calorie restaurant meals

## 2. Pepper chicken (dry) with a small portion of rice

- About **35–45 g protein** for a 150–200 g portion
- Kongu-style pepper chicken uses less gravy than most curries
- Keep the rice to one cup

## 3. Tawa fish or fish curry meals

- Mackerel (kaanaangeluthi), seer fish (vanjiram) and sardines are local and rich in protein and omega-3
- About **25–35 g protein** per fish portion
- Choose tawa-fried or curry over deep-fried

Read why mackerel is so good in our [mackerel and omega-3 guide](/blog/mackerel-benefits-omega-3-indian-diet).

## 4. Egg meals

- 3 boiled eggs or an egg curry with chapati gives about **20–30 g protein**
- Add a dal or chana side to reach 30+ g
- Cheap, quick and available everywhere

## 5. Chicken biryani (with care)

- About **25–35 g protein**, but often **700–1,000 kcal**
- Fine once in a while; ask for more chicken and less rice, and skip the extra gravy

# The best veg protein meals

## 6. Paneer or tofu tikka with chapati

- About **25–35 g protein** per meal
- Tikka is grilled; butter masala is not
- See [paneer vs tofu](/blog/paneer-vs-tofu) for which fits your goal

## 7. Dal, chana or rajma with chapati and curd

- About **20–30 g protein**
- Add a bowl of curd or a side of sprouts for more
- See [protein in dal, chana and rajma](/blog/protein-in-dal-chana-rajma)

## 8. Soya chunk dishes

- Soya chunks have about **50 g protein per 100 g dry**
- A soya pepper fry or soya biryani can pass **30 g protein** easily
- Read [soya chunks protein](/blog/soya-chunks-protein)

## 9. Kollu (horse gram) and sprouts

- A traditional Kongu protein source
- Kollu rasam, kollu sundal or sprouts salad add 8–12 g protein per bowl
- See [kollu benefits](/blog/kollu-horse-gram-benefits-weight-loss)

# Meals that look healthy but are low in protein

- **South Indian meals plate:** filling, but only about 15–20 g protein
- **Idli, dosa, pongal breakfasts:** mostly carbs; add eggs or sundal
- **Veg fried rice and noodles:** high calories, low protein
- **Fresh juices:** almost no protein

# Frequently asked questions

**How much protein should one meal have?**
For most adults, 25–40 g per main meal. Gym-goers often aim for 30–50 g.

**Is restaurant grilled chicken healthy?**
Usually yes, if it is tandoori or grilled without heavy butter basting. Ask for it less oily.

**What is the cheapest high-protein meal in Coimbatore?**
Eggs with chapati and dal, or a soya chunk dish, give the most protein per rupee.
