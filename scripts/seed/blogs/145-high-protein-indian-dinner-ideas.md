Most Indian dinners are chapati or dosa with a small curry: lots of carbs, little protein. That is why many people get hungry again at 11 PM. Here are 15 high-protein dinners that keep you full and help with fat loss or muscle gain.

# The high-protein dinner formula

1. **Main protein:** chicken, fish, eggs, paneer, tofu or soya
2. **Protein side (optional):** dal, sprouts or curd
3. **Vegetables or soup**
4. **Light carb:** 1–2 chapatis, 1–2 dosas or ½–1 cup rice

# Non-veg dinners

## 1. Pepper chicken with dosa

150 g dry pepper chicken, 2 plain dosas, a small salad.
**About 40 g protein.** Recipe idea: [pepper chicken steak](/blog/pepper-chicken-steak-recipe).

## 2. Grilled fish with rasam rice

150 g tawa or grilled fish, ¾ cup rice with rasam, poriyal.
**About 30–35 g protein.** See [grilled mackerel](/blog/grilled-mackerel-recipe).

## 3. Egg curry with chapati and sprouts

3-egg curry, 2 chapatis, a cup of sprouts.
**About 28–30 g protein.**

## 4. Chicken soup with chapati

A big bowl of chicken and vegetable soup (150 g chicken), 1–2 chapatis.
**About 40 g protein.** Light and great for weight loss.

## 5. Smashed chicken with oats upma

150 g [smashed chicken](/blog/smashed-chicken-bowl-recipe) with a small bowl of oats upma.
**About 50 g protein.**

## 6. Chicken tikka salad bowl

150 g chicken tikka over cucumber, onion, tomato, sweet corn and lemon, with a spoon of curd dressing.
**About 45 g protein.**

## 7. Egg bhurji wraps

3-egg bhurji rolled in 2 chapatis with onion and mint chutney.
**About 25 g protein.**

# Veg dinners

## 8. Paneer bhurji with chapati

100 g paneer bhurji with peas and capsicum, 2 chapatis.
**About 25–30 g protein.**

## 9. Soya keema with chapati

50 g dry soya granules cooked like keema, 2 chapatis.
**About 28–30 g protein.** See [smashed soya bowl](/blog/smashed-soya-bowl-recipe).

## 10. Tofu tikka with dal

150 g [tofu tikka](/blog/tandoori-tofu-tikka-recipe), a bowl of dal, salad.
**About 25–30 g protein.**

## 11. Dal khichdi with curd and boiled eggs

Moong dal khichdi (more dal than rice), a cup of curd, 2 boiled eggs (or paneer cubes for veg).
**About 25 g protein.**

## 12. Paneer tikka with sundal

100 g paneer tikka, a cup of [black chana sundal](/blog/black-chana-sundal-recipe), salad.
**About 30 g protein.**

## 13. Rajma or chana with millet roti

1.5 cups rajma or chana, 2 ragi or jowar rotis, onion salad.
**About 20–22 g protein.**

## 14. Moong dal chilla with paneer filling

3 chillas with grated paneer, mint chutney, curd.
**About 25 g protein.**

## 15. Pepper pongal oats with eggs

A bowl of oats and moong dal pongal, 2 boiled eggs.
**About 20–22 g protein.**

# Tips for better dinners

- **Smaller carb portion than lunch** if you are losing weight
- **Protein first** on the plate
- **Avoid very oily or spicy food late** if you get acidity
- **Finish 2–3 hours before bed** when possible. See [eating late at night and weight loss](/blog/eating-late-at-night-weight-loss).
- **Cook protein ahead** with [weekly meal prep](/blog/weekly-meal-prep-indian)

# Frequently asked questions

**Is it okay to eat chapati at night?**
Yes. 1–2 chapatis with a protein dish is a good dinner.

**Should dinner be the smallest meal?**
It helps many people, but total daily calories matter most.

**Is paneer good at night?**
Yes. It is a slow-digesting protein. See [protein before bed](/blog/protein-before-bed).
