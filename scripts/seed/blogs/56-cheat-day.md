> **Quick answer:** A **cheat meal once a week** fits easily into most fat-loss plans. A full **cheat day** often does not: a day of 3,500–5,000 kcal can wipe out most or all of a week's calorie deficit. If you want a treat, plan **one meal**, eat it slowly, keep protein in the rest of the day, and go straight back to normal at the next meal.

"I was good all week, so Sunday is cheat day." It sounds fair. But many people who diet Monday to Saturday and cheat on Sunday end up wondering why the scale has not moved in a month.

Here is the maths, and how to enjoy food you love without undoing your progress.

# The cheat day maths

Say you eat **500 kcal below maintenance** for 6 days. That is a weekly deficit of:

- 6 × 500 = **3,000 kcal**

Now imagine a typical cheat day:

- Breakfast: 3 parottas with salna, about 900 kcal
- Lunch: chicken biryani with chicken 65, about 1,400 kcal
- Evening: bakery puffs and a cool drink, about 600 kcal
- Dinner: pizza or shawarma and dessert, about 1,500 kcal

That is about **4,400 kcal**. If your maintenance is about 2,200 kcal, you are **2,200 kcal over** for the day. Your weekly deficit drops from 3,000 to about 800 kcal.

Do this every week, and fat loss slows to almost nothing.

# Cheat meal vs cheat day

**Cheat meal**
- One planned meal of food you enjoy
- Usually 800–1,500 kcal
- Uses up a part of your weekly deficit
- Easy to bounce back from

**Cheat day**
- A whole day with no limits
- Often 3,500–5,000 kcal or more
- Can erase most or all of the week's deficit
- Often turns into a "cheat weekend"

For most people, a cheat **meal** is the better choice.

# Do cheat days "boost metabolism"?

You may have heard that a cheat day "shocks" your metabolism. The truth:

- A big day of eating may slightly raise your energy use for a short time, but not enough to make up for the extra calories.
- Some research on planned "diet breaks" (eating at maintenance for 1–2 weeks) shows benefits for longer diets. That is very different from a one-day binge.

# Why the scale jumps after a cheat day

After a big meal or day, the scale often jumps 1–2 kg. Most of that is:

- **Water** held with extra carbs and salt
- **Food** still in your digestive system

It is **not** 2 kg of fat. It usually drops back within 2–4 days if you return to normal eating. Our [weight loss plateau guide](/blog/weight-loss-plateau-why-scale-stopped) explains water weight and other reasons the scale lies.

# How to have a cheat meal the smart way

1. **Plan it.** Choose the day and the meal. Unplanned "cheats" add up fast.
2. **Eat protein in your other meals** that day to stay full and keep your total in check.
3. **Do not starve before it.** Skipping meals often leads to overeating later.
4. **Choose what you really want.** Enjoy your favourite biryani, not random packet snacks.
5. **Eat slowly.** You will enjoy it more and eat less.
6. **Skip liquid calories.** Soft drinks and shakes add a lot without filling you up.
7. **Go back to normal at the next meal.** Not "from Monday".

# How often should you have a cheat meal?

- **Fat loss:** about once a week works for most people.
- **Maintenance:** 2–3 relaxed meals a week is usually fine.
- **Muscle gain:** treats fit easily as long as most food is good quality.

Life events like weddings and festivals are special cases. Our [wedding season guide](/blog/wedding-season-diet-tamil-nadu) and [Deepavali sweets guide](/blog/deepavali-sweets-calories-guide) show how to handle them.

# "I binged. What now?"

It happens to everyone.

- **Do not punish yourself** with fasting or hours of cardio.
- **Return to your normal meals** at the next meal.
- **Drink water** and get some activity like a walk.
- **Look at your weekly average**, not one day.

One bad day does not ruin progress. A bad week after a bad day does.

# Frequently asked questions

**Is one cheat meal a week okay for weight loss?**
Yes, for most people. Plan it and keep it to one meal.

**Should I call it a "cheat" meal at all?**
Some people find "free meal" or "planned meal" better. It reduces guilt and the urge to binge.

**Can I have a cheat meal during a 75 Hard challenge?**
No. The original 75 Hard rules ban cheat meals. See [75 Hard challenge](/blog/75-hard-challenge).

**Will one cheat day ruin my week?**
It can undo much of a week's deficit, but it will not erase your progress. Get back on track the next day.
