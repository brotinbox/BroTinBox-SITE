> **Quick answer:** For **tandoori paneer tikka** at home, marinate **250 g paneer cubes** with **hung curd, roasted besan, Kashmiri chilli, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, kasuri methi, lemon and 1 tsp mustard oil** for 30 minutes, thread with onion and capsicum, then cook on a **hot tawa for 2–3 minutes per side** or in the **oven at 220°C for 12–15 minutes**. A **125 g paneer serving** gives about **23–25 g protein and 370–440 kcal**.

Paneer tikka is one of the easiest high-protein veg dishes to make at home, and you do not need a tandoor. This is a home-style version of the **Tandoori Paneer Tikka** on our BroTin Box veg menu.

# Ingredients (serves 2)

**Paneer and vegetables**

- 250 g fresh paneer, cut into 3 cm cubes
- 1 onion, cut into petals
- 1 capsicum, cut into squares

**Marinade**

- ½ cup thick hung curd
- 1 tbsp besan (gram flour), dry roasted for 1 minute until fragrant
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder (for colour)
- ½ tsp regular chilli powder (optional, for heat)
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp kasuri methi, crushed between your palms
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp mustard oil (or any oil)
- Salt to taste

# Method

## 1. Make the marinade

Mix the hung curd, roasted besan, spices, kasuri methi, lemon juice, oil and salt into a thick paste. It should coat a spoon without dripping.

**Why roasted besan?** It soaks up moisture and helps the marinade cling to the paneer, so you get those charred edges instead of a watery coating.

## 2. Marinate

Add the paneer, onion and capsicum. Mix gently so the paneer does not break. Rest for **30 minutes** (up to 2 hours in the fridge).

## 3. Thread

Thread the paneer, onion and capsicum onto skewers. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 20 minutes first so they do not burn.

## 4. Cook

**On a tawa or grill pan**

- Heat on medium-high and brush with a few drops of oil.
- Cook the skewers (or loose pieces) for **2–3 minutes per side** until charred spots appear.

**In the oven**

- Preheat to 220°C.
- Place skewers on a rack over a tray.
- Bake **12–15 minutes**, turning once, then grill for 2 minutes for extra colour.

**In an air fryer**

- 200°C for **8–10 minutes**, turning halfway.

## 5. Finish

Sprinkle chaat masala and a squeeze of lemon. Serve with mint chutney and onion rings.

# Nutrition per serving

For 125 g full-fat paneer with marinade and vegetables:

- **Calories:** about 370–440 kcal
- **Protein:** about 23–25 g
- **Fat:** about 26–32 g
- **Carbs:** about 10–12 g

**Lighter version:** use **low-fat paneer**. It brings protein up to about 25–30 g and calories down to about 250–300 kcal per serving.

- **Paneer tikka** (23–25 g protein)
- **A bowl of chana or rajma** (8–10 g)
- **Cucumber or sweet corn salad**
- **2 chapatis**

That gives about **35–40 g protein** in one meal.

# Tips for restaurant-style tikka

- **Use firm paneer.** Very soft paneer breaks on skewers. Press it for 10 minutes if it is wet.
- **Do not skip the hung curd.** Regular curd makes the marinade runny. Hang curd in a cloth for 1–2 hours.
- **High heat, short time.** Overcooked paneer turns rubbery.
- **Smoky flavour without a tandoor:** place a small steel bowl with a hot piece of charcoal and a few drops of ghee in the pan, cover for 2 minutes (the "dhungar" method). Do this in a ventilated kitchen.

# Variations

- **Tofu tikka:** use pressed firm tofu, same marinade, 30 minutes. Fewer calories, similar method.
- **Mint paneer tikka:** add 2 tbsp mint-coriander paste to the marinade.
- **Malai style:** skip the chilli and add a little cream and cashew paste (higher calories).

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I make paneer tikka without an oven?**
Yes. A tawa, grill pan or air fryer works well.

**Is paneer tikka good for weight loss?**
In a measured portion, yes. It is grilled, not fried. Use low-fat paneer if calories are tight.

**How long does it keep?**
Up to 2 days in the fridge. Reheat on a tawa, not in the microwave.
