> **Quick answer:** Gaining **5 kg in 60 days** is possible for many **underweight or very skinny** people with a surplus of about **500–700 kcal a day** and strength training, though part of it will be fat and water. Gaining **10 kg in 60 days** is unrealistic for healthy weight gain unless you are recovering from illness or were very underweight. For mostly lean weight, aim for **2–4 kg in 60 days** (about **0.25–0.5 kg a week**).

"How do I gain weight fast?" is one of the most common questions from skinny guys and girls in India. Here is how much you can realistically gain in two months, and a plan to do it without just adding belly fat.

# What is realistic in 60 days?

- **Lean gain (mostly muscle, some fat):** about **2–4 kg**
- **Faster gain (muscle, more fat, water):** about **4–6 kg**, best suited to underweight people
- **10 kg:** usually means a lot of fat, or regaining weight lost during an illness

Why the limit? Your body can only build muscle at a certain speed. Beginners gain about **0.5–1 kg of muscle a month**. Anything beyond that is mostly fat, water and food in your gut.

# Who should aim for 5 kg or more?

- **BMI under 18.5** (underweight)
- Very skinny people who have always struggled to gain
- People regaining weight after illness, under medical advice

If your BMI is normal, aim for the lean 2–4 kg range. You will look better at the end.

# Step 1: Find your surplus

1. Estimate maintenance: body weight (kg) × 32–36 if you are active and skinny (fast metabolisms are common in "hard gainers")
2. Add **400–500 kcal** for a lean gain, or **500–700 kcal** if you are underweight and aiming for 5 kg

**Example:** a 55 kg student. Maintenance about 55 × 34 = 1,870 kcal. Target: **about 2,400–2,500 kcal a day**.

# Step 2: Get protein right

**1.6–2.2 g per kg** of body weight. For 55 kg, that is about **90–120 g a day**.

Spread it over 4–5 meals. See [how much protein per day](/blog/how-much-protein-per-day).

# Step 3: Make eating easier

Skinny people often feel full quickly. These tricks help:

- **Eat every 3–4 hours**, even if you are not very hungry
- **Drink some calories:** milk, banana milkshake, lassi, homemade protein shakes
- **Add calorie-dense foods:** peanut butter, nuts, ghee in moderation, paneer, curd rice, dates
- **Bigger plates, not more meals:** an extra chapati or half cup of rice adds up
- **Do not fill up on water** just before meals

See [protein shakes without protein powder](/blog/protein-shake-without-protein-powder) for easy shake recipes, and [peanut butter for muscle gain](/blog/peanut-butter-for-muscle-gain).

# Step 4: Lift weights

Without training, a surplus mostly turns into fat. Lift **3–5 days a week** with compound exercises: squats, bench press, rows, overhead press, deadlifts.

Follow our [push pull legs plan](/blog/push-pull-legs-workout-plan) or the month-by-month guide in [how to bulk in 60 days](/blog/how-to-bulk-in-60-days).

# A sample 2,500 kcal day

- **7 AM:** 3-egg omelette, 2 chapatis with peanut butter, a glass of milk
- **10:30 AM:** banana milkshake with oats and dates
- **1 PM:** 150 g chicken or paneer, 1.5 cups rice, dal, vegetables, curd
- **5 PM (pre-workout):** 2 bananas and a handful of peanuts
- **8 PM:** fish or soya chunks curry, 3 chapatis, vegetables
- **10 PM:** a glass of milk

About **2,500 kcal and 110–120 g protein**.

# Week-by-week targets

- **Weeks 1–2:** +1–1.5 kg (some is water and food volume)
- **Weeks 3–8:** +0.4–0.6 kg a week
- **Day 60:** +4–5 kg for an underweight person on the faster plan, or +2–4 kg on the lean plan

If you gain faster than 0.75 kg a week after the first two weeks, cut 200 kcal. If you stall for 2 weeks, add 200–300 kcal.

# Avoid these "fast weight gain" traps

- **Mass gainers full of sugar:** cheap calories, mostly carbs. Real food is better.
- **Junk food bulking:** parotta, biryani, bakery items every day. Read [dirty bulk vs clean bulk](/blog/dirty-bulk-vs-clean-bulk).
- **"Weight gain" tablets or powders with steroids:** some sold online and in shops contain hidden steroids. Avoid anything that promises quick weight gain without food.
- **Skipping training.**

# When to see a doctor

If you eat a lot but cannot gain at all, or you are losing weight without trying, see a doctor. Thyroid problems, diabetes, worms, digestive issues and other conditions can cause it.

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I gain 10 kg in 2 months?**
Not as healthy, lean weight for most people. You might gain 10 kg on the scale with a big surplus, but much of it would be fat.

**Is it okay to gain weight without the gym?**
You will gain weight, but more of it will be fat. Even home workouts with push-ups and squats help.

**What is the best food for weight gain?**
Whole foods with protein and calories: milk, eggs, chicken, paneer, curd, peanuts, bananas, rice, chapati and dal.
