> **Quick answer:** To make **mint grilled chicken** at home, marinate **500 g boneless chicken** in **hung curd, fresh mint, coriander, green chilli, ginger, garlic, lemon, pepper and cumin** for 1–4 hours, then cook on a hot grill pan or tawa with **1 tsp oil** for about **6–7 minutes per side** until fully cooked. A **150 g cooked portion** of chicken breast gives about **45 g protein and 260–300 kcal**.

Mint grilled chicken is one of the most popular dishes in our BroTin Box menu. It is fresh, high in protein and easy to make at home with ingredients you already have. Here is a home-style version you can cook in your own kitchen.

# Ingredients (serves 3)

**Chicken**

- 500 g boneless chicken breast or thigh, cut into large flat pieces

**Mint marinade**

- 1 cup fresh mint leaves
- ½ cup fresh coriander leaves
- 2–3 green chillies (adjust to taste)
- 1 inch ginger
- 5–6 garlic cloves
- 4 tbsp thick curd or hung curd
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp black pepper powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Salt to taste

**For cooking**

- 1 tsp oil

# Method

## 1. Make the mint paste

Grind the mint, coriander, green chillies, ginger and garlic with 1–2 tbsp water into a smooth paste.

## 2. Mix the marinade

In a bowl, mix the mint paste with curd, lemon juice, cumin, pepper, turmeric and salt.

**Tip:** use thick or hung curd. Watery curd makes the chicken steam instead of grill.

## 3. Prepare the chicken

- Pat the chicken dry.
- If using breast, flatten thick pieces with a rolling pin to about 1.5 cm, so they cook evenly.
- Make a few shallow cuts on the surface.

## 4. Marinate

Coat the chicken well. Cover and keep in the fridge for **at least 1 hour**, up to 4 hours. Do not marinate overnight with lemon; the chicken can turn mushy.

## 5. Grill

- Heat a grill pan or heavy tawa on medium-high heat.
- Brush with 1 tsp oil.
- Shake off extra marinade and place the chicken on the pan.
- Cook **6–7 minutes on the first side** without moving it.
- Flip and cook **5–6 minutes more**.
- Cut the thickest piece: it should be **white all the way through** with clear juices. If you have a thermometer, it should read 74°C inside.

## 6. Rest and serve

Rest for 3–5 minutes before slicing, so the juices stay inside. Serve with lemon wedges, onion rings and mint chutney.

# Nutrition per serving

For about **150 g cooked chicken breast** (one of three servings):

- **Calories:** about 260–300 kcal
- **Protein:** about 45 g
- **Fat:** about 6–9 g
- **Carbs:** about 3–4 g

Using **thigh** makes it juicier, with slightly less protein and more fat (about 35–40 g protein and 12–15 g fat per serving).

# Tips for the best result

- **Do not crowd the pan.** Cook in batches, or the chicken releases water and boils.
- **Do not flip too early.** Let a crust form before turning.
- **Use the grill or oven:** you can also bake at 220°C for 18–22 minutes, or air fry at 200°C for 14–16 minutes, turning halfway. Compare methods in [grilled vs air fried](/blog/grilled-vs-air-fried).
- **Make it ahead:** keeps for 2 days in the fridge in a sealed box. Reheat on a pan, not in the microwave, to keep it juicy.

# Variations

- **Spicy:** add 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder and 1 extra green chilli
- **Veg version:** use paneer or pressed tofu; marinate for 30 minutes and grill for 3–4 minutes per side
- **Kids' version:** skip the green chilli and add a little honey to the marinade

# Frequently asked questions

**Can I use frozen chicken?**
Yes. Thaw it fully in the fridge and pat it dry before marinating.

**Why is my grilled chicken dry?**
Usually it is overcooked or the pieces are too thin or uneven. Flatten to an even thickness and stop cooking as soon as it is done.

**Is mint grilled chicken good for weight loss?**
Yes. It is high in protein, low in fat and full of flavour without heavy gravy.
